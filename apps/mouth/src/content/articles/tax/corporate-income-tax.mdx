## SME Final Tax (PP 55/2022 & PP 20/2026 Exclusion)

<InfoCard variant="warning" title="CRITICAL UPDATE — PP 20/2026 (Effective 22 April 2026)">
Under **PP 55/2022**, taxpayers with gross turnover under **IDR 4.8 billion/year** could access a 0.5% final tax rate with maximum windows of:

- **3 years** for standard limited companies (**PT**)
- **4 years** for **CV, Firma, and Koperasi**
- **7 years** for individual taxpayers (**Orang Pribadi**)

**Important PP 20/2026 Update (effective 22 April 2026):** Multi-shareholder **PTs, PT PMAs, CVs, and Firmas** are **excluded** from newly entering the 0.5% final tax regime going forward. Pre-existing domestic PTs/CVs already using PP 55/2022 before PP 20/2026 finish only their remaining 3-year/4-year window; **PT Perorangan** and cooperatives remain subject to statutory eligibility, while individual taxpayers (**Orang Pribadi**) retain the 0.5% rate without the 7-year time limit. Standard PT PMAs with turnover up to **IDR 50 billion** instead apply **UU HPP Art. 31E** (11% effective CIT on the portion proportional to IDR 4.8B turnover, 22% on the balance).

</InfoCard>

### Eligible 0.5% Final Regime Illustration (Grandfathered / Solo)

| --------------- | ------------------ |
| IDR 100M        | IDR 500K           |
| IDR 200M        | IDR 1M             |

## Transfer Pricing

<InfoCard variant="warning" title="Related Party Transactions">
Indonesia has strict **transfer pricing** rules for transactions between related parties:

- Must be at **arm's length** prices
- **Documentation required** (Master File, Local File)
- **Country-by-Country Reporting** for large groups
- **Penalties** for non-compliance

Work with qualified tax advisors for related party transactions.

</InfoCard>

### Documentation Requirements

| Report      | Required For        | Threshold                |
| ----------- | ------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Master File | Large taxpayers     | Revenue IDR 50B+         |
| Local File  | Related party deals | Any material transaction |
| CbC Report  | Multinationals      | Group revenue €750M+     |
