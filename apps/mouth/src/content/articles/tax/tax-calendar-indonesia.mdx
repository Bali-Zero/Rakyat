# Indonesia Tax Calendar 2026: Every Deadline for Individuals & Businesses

Stay compliant with Indonesian tax law by knowing your deadlines. Here's the complete tax calendar.

<KeyTakeaway>
  **TL;DR:** Under **PMK 81/2024**, Indonesia's monthly PPh payments (PPh 21,
  23, 25, 26, and 4(2)) are harmonized to the **15th of the following month**,
  with SPT Masa PPh returns due by the **20th**. VAT (PPN) payment and SPT
  Masa PPN filing are due by the **end of the following month**. Annual SPT
  Tahunan is due March 31 (individuals) or April 30 (corporates). Late
  payments incur variable monthly **KMK interest penalties** (max 24 months).
</KeyTakeaway>

<HeaderWhatsAppCTA />

<InfoCard variant="warning" title="2026 Key Updates (Source: pajak.go.id)">
**Key Tax Administration Updates for 2026:**

1. **Coretax System** — Indonesia's integrated Core Tax Administration System (Coretax) is live; monthly e-Billing, e-Faktur, e-Bupot, and tax returns are consolidated in `coretaxdjp.pajak.go.id`.
2. **PMK 81/2024 Harmonized Payment Date** — Monthly PPh payment deadlines (PPh 21, 23, 25, 26, and 4(2)) are unified on the **15th of the following month**, and monthly SPT Masa filing remains the **20th**.
3. **VAT (PPN) 12% Nominal / 11% Effective (PMK 131/2024)** — The statutory PPN rate is 12%, but non-luxury general goods and services use the `11/12` DPP Nilai Lain tax base for an **11% effective rate**; only statutory luxury goods pay the full 12% rate.
4. **NIK as NPWP (16-Digit TIN)** — Individual taxpayers use their 16-digit NIK as NPWP (with 16-digit / NITKU numbering under Coretax).

</InfoCard>

## Filing Methods

### E-Filing Options

| System      | Purpose                         | Website               |
| ----------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| **Coretax** | Integrated DJP tax administration system (2025+) | coretaxdjp.pajak.go.id |
| DJP Online  | Legacy portal (prior-year returns/reference)    | djponline.pajak.go.id |
| e-Billing   | Tax payment billing code                        | Integrated in Coretax |
| e-Faktur    | VAT e-invoicing                                 | Integrated in Coretax |
| e-Bupot     | Withholding certificates (Bukti Potong)         | Integrated in Coretax |

<InfoCard variant="note" title="Transition to Coretax">

  Since 2025, DJP's **Coretax** portal (`coretaxdjp.pajak.go.id`) has replaced
  separate legacy e-Filing, e-Billing, e-Faktur, and e-Bupot interfaces for
  2025+ tax periods. All active corporate and individual taxpayers must activate
  and maintain their Coretax access for monthly and annual compliance.
</InfoCard>

## Late Filing Penalties

### SPT Filing Penalties

| Return Type                  | Penalty               |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Monthly SPT (Masa PPh)       | IDR 100,000 per month |
| Annual Personal (PPh OP)     | IDR 100,000           |
| Annual Corporate (PPh Badan) | IDR 1,000,000         |
| Monthly VAT (SPT Masa PPN)   | IDR 500,000           |

### Late Payment Penalties

| Violation               | Penalty                                                              |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Late tax payment        | Variable monthly KMK interest rate under UU HPP (max 24 months)      |
| Underpayment discovered | Variable monthly KMK rate + administrative surcharge per KUP/UU HPP  |
