## The Facts

Two reported overstay detentions in Denpasar are a reminder to check the permission that governs your current stay in Indonesia. A visa document may contain more than one date, and they should not be treated as interchangeable.

## What authorities reported

Okezone reported on 24 November 2026 that Denpasar Immigration had detained two Ghanaian nationals following a patrol on 20 November. The report, quoting office head R. Haryo Sakti, gives alleged overstay periods of 245 and 530 days.

It says both people were taken to the immigration office for further examination. That account does not establish that deportation had already been completed. The case should not be used to generalize about a nationality or about all foreign residents.

## Visa validity and stay permission are different

The official Indonesian eVisa user guide explains that a visa's validity period differs from its period of stay. The date by which a visa may be used for entry is not automatically the date until which a person can remain after entering.

An individual review should use the actual visa category, arrival record, current stay permit and any approved extension. Do not calculate a deadline from a social-media example or assume another traveller's documents follow the same rules.

## A short record check for expats

Keep the following together:

- The visa issued for the trip.
- The arrival or entry record.
- The current stay-permit document and its expiry.
- Any extension approval and associated receipt.
- Contact details for the party assisting with the application.
- Written clarification of discrepancies or unresolved application status.

If somebody is managing the process for you, request the actual approval document. A message saying an application is being handled is not the same evidence as an issued approval.

Set reminders before the documented expiry, leaving time to clarify requirements with the responsible immigration office. If a date is already in doubt, seek official clarification promptly rather than assuming that a flight booking or a pending request resolves it.

## For villa and business operators

A host or employer can encourage accurate records without making immigration decisions for a guest or employee. Keep any information collected secure and share it only through the appropriate authorized process.

The practical lesson is document discipline. This report does not announce a new overstay rule or establish the outcome of every case. The applicable response depends on the person's actual status and the authorities' assessment.
