## The Facts

ANTARA reported on 21 November 2026, citing Ngurah Rai Immigration head Muhamad Novyandri, that a Russian national identified by the initials IG was refused entry after an alert connected to an INTERPOL Red Notice. The report said he was removed to Moscow on 18 November following inter-agency coordination.

The reported underlying matter concerns alleged embezzlement and fraud. This article does not treat those allegations as a conviction. The report does not describe the case as an ordinary overstay.

INTERPOL explains that a Red Notice is a request for international law-enforcement cooperation to locate and provisionally arrest a person pending extradition, surrender or similar action. It is not itself an international arrest warrant; countries act under their own laws.

---

## In Practice

For readers, the useful lesson is to identify the actual basis of an immigration story before repeating it. Entry refusal, an expired permission and a criminal allegation are different issues. A dramatic deportation headline does not show that all travellers from the same country face the same situation.

If a traveller has a real concern about a legal proceeding or an adverse immigration record, obtain qualified advice about that specific matter before travel. Do not assume that a new visa application resolves an unrelated legal issue.

Likewise, avoid treating the absence of a name from a public internet search as proof that no law-enforcement information exists. INTERPOL notes that most Red Notices are restricted to law-enforcement use.

---

## What This Does Not Establish

The available reporting does not establish a new blanket entry restriction for Russian citizens or a finding of guilt against the individual. The case illustrates a particular border intervention, not a general rule for every expat.
