# E-VOA Indonesia 2026: B1 Visa on Arrival Guide

The **B1 e-VOA (Electronic Visa on Arrival)** permits an initial stay of **up to 30 days from arrival**. You may apply for **one extension of up to 30 days**, giving **60 days total**. The extension is not automatic. [Official B1 rules](https://jakartapusat.imigrasi.go.id/layanan/warga-negara-asing-wna/visa-republik-indonesia/b1-visa-saat-kedatangan-wisata).

This guide covers online applications, eligibility, documents, extension procedures and the distinction between B1, visa-free entry and C1.

## What is E-VOA?

E-VOA is the electronic application route for B1. It is not a separate 60-day visa. B1 is single-entry: leaving Indonesia ends that admission. Eligible travellers can also obtain VOA at designated arrival checkpoints; online application has not abolished airport VOA. [Official B1 rules](https://jakartapusat.imigrasi.go.id/layanan/warga-negara-asing-wna/visa-republik-indonesia/b1-visa-saat-kedatangan-wisata).

- **Initial stay:** up to 30 days.
- **Extension:** once, up to 30 more days; maximum 60 days from arrival.
- **Application portal:** [evisa.imigrasi.go.id](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id).
- **Work:** B1 is not a work authorisation.

## Who Can Apply for E-VOA?

Eligibility depends on nationality, travel document and purpose. Check the current official list rather than relying on a country count copied into an article. Some nationalities qualify for both visa-free entry and B1; visa-free eligibility does not exclude a B1 application.

If your nationality is not eligible, check the appropriate visa route before booking. Do not assume that an old B211A label describes the current visa you need. [Official visa and nationality information](https://ngurahrai.imigrasi.go.id/Layanan%20WNA/).

## How to Apply for E-VOA Online

1. Open the [official eVisa portal](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id) and check the B1 requirements for your nationality and travel document.
2. Prepare a passport with at least six months' validity from arrival, a clear biographical-page scan, a recent digital photo and a return/onward ticket. Follow the current portal checklist for any other documents.
3. Follow the portal's application or account flow. Enter your full name exactly as in your passport, date of birth, nationality and passport number.
4. Enter your intended arrival details, purpose and accommodation address accurately. Upload readable files in the formats requested by the portal.
5. Check every field before submitting. Pay the current official bill only through the payment flow provided by the portal.
6. Check application status and save the issued e-VOA. Keep a digital backup and a printed copy available for your journey.

**Document checks:** Confirm passport expiry, spelling and passport number before submitting. Check that the photo and passport scan are clear. Do not assume a payment receipt is an issued visa or that approval is guaranteed within a fixed number of hours.

Plan your departure around your authorised stay. A future extension is a request, not an entitlement. Confirm ticket and entry requirements with the carrier and immigration before travel. [B1 application requirements](https://jakartapusat.imigrasi.go.id/layanan/warga-negara-asing-wna/visa-republik-indonesia/b1-visa-saat-kedatangan-wisata).

## Arrival Procedure at Indonesian Airport

Have your passport, issued e-VOA and return/onward ticket available. Follow the airport's current signs and immigration instructions. Check the actual stay-permit expiry recorded on entry; the visa's entry-validity window is not the permitted length of stay.

If you have not obtained e-VOA, check whether your nationality and arrival checkpoint permit VOA or visa-free entry. Do not assume either route is available to everyone. [Official arrival routes](https://ngurahrai.imigrasi.go.id/Layanan%20WNA/).

## E-VOA Extension: From 30 to 60 Days

| Stage                  | Permitted stay             |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Initial B1 stay        | Up to 30 days from arrival |
| One approved extension | Up to 30 additional days   |
| Maximum total          | 60 days from arrival       |

There is no second B1 extension.

### How to Extend E-VOA

Ngurah Rai's published procedure starts through the [official stay-permit claim page](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/web/applications/stay-permit/claim). Update your actual accommodation address, submit the requested documents, pay the official bill and follow the appointment instructions. Expect an **immigration-office visit for a photo and interview**. An online application is not a promise of a fully online extension.

**Before paying or handing over documents, reconfirm the procedure, required documents and appointment with the office responsible for your address.** Timing and local implementation can change. An agent can help prepare documents and communicate with the office; an agent cannot guarantee approval or remove your attendance obligations. [Ngurah Rai extension instructions](https://ngurahrai.imigrasi.go.id/Layanan%20WNA/).

### Extension Deadlines

Ngurah Rai publishes filing no later than **seven days before expiry**. Reconfirm this with the office handling your application; do not present one office's timetable as a universal rule. Check your recorded expiry and arrange filing before that deadline. Do not rely on last-minute rescue, backdating or submission alone as proof of an extended stay. [Published local instructions](https://ngurahrai.imigrasi.go.id/Layanan%20WNA/).

## E-VOA vs Visa-Free Entry vs C1

| Route                 | Initial stay                        | Extension                                      |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| B1 VOA / e-VOA        | Up to 30 days                       | Once, up to 30 more days; 60 total             |
| Visa-free entry       | Up to 30 days for eligible visitors | Not extendable                                 |
| C1 tourism visit visa | Up to 60 days                       | Separate extension rules; up to 180 days total |

C1 is a different visa, not a longer B1. Check its application requirements before travel. An electronic application does not make every eVisa an e-VOA. Historical B211A terminology should not replace checking the current visa index. [C1 rules](https://jakartapusat.imigrasi.go.id/layanan/warga-negara-asing-wna/visa-republik-indonesia/c1-visa-wisata), [visa-free rules](https://ngurahrai.imigrasi.go.id/Layanan%20WNA/).

Choose B1 for an eligible short visit, with one extension if approved. For a longer stay or a different activity, assess the appropriate visa rather than planning repeated B1 extensions or assuming border runs guarantee re-entry.

## Common E-VOA Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

- Confusing the visa's entry-validity window with the stay authorised on arrival.
- Planning 60 days without applying for the one available extension.
- Copying a name or passport number incorrectly or uploading unreadable files.
- Treating online filing as exemption from an office appointment.
- Treating a tourist visa as permission to work remotely.
- Waiting until the recorded expiry to organise the extension.

## E-VOA FAQs

### Can I enter without e-VOA?

Eligible travellers may use VOA at designated checkpoints or visa-free entry where applicable. Check the route for your nationality before travel; visa-free entry cannot be extended. [Arrival options](https://ngurahrai.imigrasi.go.id/Layanan%20WNA/).

### Can I work remotely on E-VOA?

No. Do not rely on claims that remote work is a tolerated grey area. Assess the correct activity-specific visa, including E33G where relevant, before working. Eligibility must be checked for your circumstances.

### What if my stay expires?

Contact immigration promptly. Do not rely on a purported fine cap, an assumed grace period or an agent's rescue promise. An application is not an automatic extension.

### Can I leave and re-enter to reset B1?

Departure ends the single-entry admission. Any later visit needs its own valid entry basis and remains subject to immigration checks. A border run is not a guaranteed extension.

### Can I change from B1 to another stay permit?

This is case-dependent. Check the current immigration route, category and residence history before committing money or documents. Neither automatic conversion nor a blanket claim that conversion is always impossible is reliable for every case. [B1 information](https://jakartapusat.imigrasi.go.id/layanan/warga-negara-asing-wna/visa-republik-indonesia/b1-visa-saat-kedatangan-wisata).

### Can children travel on the same application?

Check the immigration requirements for each traveller, including infants. An airline's child-fare policy does not create a visa exemption.

### What if my application is rejected?

Read the portal's reason and instructions. Correct inaccurate or incomplete information only through the permitted process. Do not assume another payment guarantees approval or that a previous fee will be refunded.
