### Minimum Thresholds

| KITAS Type                   | Investment / Shareholding Requirement                      |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Investor KITAS (E28A)        | IDR 10,000,000,000 minimum personal shareholding in PT PMA |
| Director / Working KITAS (E23) | No personal shareholding minimum (requires RPTKA + DKP-TKA) |
| Employee KITAS (E23)         | Not shareholding-based (requires RPTKA + DKP-TKA)          |

## Pure Investor (No Work)

If you're a passive shareholder:

### What You CAN Do

- Attend annual general meetings
- Participate in shareholder decisions
- Receive dividends
- Review company reports
- Visit your investment

### What You CANNOT Do

- Manage daily operations
- Direct employees
- Sign contracts as company rep
- Represent company to clients
- Make operational decisions

<InfoCard variant="warning" title="Line Between Active and Passive">

Immigration can question if you're "really" just an investor if you're
constantly at the office. Keep your activities clearly passive if using
Investor KITAS.

</InfoCard>

## Documents for Shareholder KITAS

### All Shareholders Need

- Passport (24+ months validity)
- Passport photos (4x6, red background)
- CV/Resume
- Company deed showing shareholding

### For Investor KITAS (E28A)

- Proof of paid-up share ownership (IDR 10B+ own shareholding)
- Company deed (Akta) and Kemenkumham SK showing shareholding
- NIB and BKPM/OSS company documents

### For Working Shareholder / Director (E23)

- All identity and company documents
- RPTKA approval and DKP-TKA levy payment
- Job description
- Qualifications (degree, experience)

## Multiple Shareholders

If your PT PMA has multiple foreign shareholders:

| Foreign Shareholders Seeking E28A | Own Shareholding per Applicant | Minimum Total Share Capital |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| 1 E28A applicant                  | IDR 10B+                       | Company paid-up min IDR 2.5B (E28A requires IDR 10B own shares) |
| 2 E28A applicants                 | IDR 10B+ each = IDR 20B+       | IDR 20B+ total shares       |
| 3 E28A applicants                 | IDR 10B+ each = IDR 30B+       | IDR 30B+ total shares       |

<InfoCard variant="note" title="Each E28A Applicant Needs IDR 10B Own Shareholding">

For E28A Investor KITAS eligibility, each applicant must hold at least **IDR 10 billion** in personal shares in the sponsoring PT PMA (two people cannot split a single IDR 10B holding to obtain two E28A permits). Shareholders with smaller stakes who manage operations should apply for **E23 Working KITAS** instead.

</InfoCard>

## Shareholder Without KITAS?

Not all shareholders need KITAS:

### When KITAS Not Required

- You live outside Indonesia
- You visit on tourist/business visa only
- You don't need to work in Indonesia
- You only attend occasional meetings

### When KITAS Recommended

- You want to live in Indonesia
- You need to be present regularly
- You want to manage the business
- You want stability/long-term stay

## Path to Permanent Residency

Shareholders can work toward KITAP (permanent stay):

1. **3 consecutive years on KITAS** (same category)
2. **Good compliance record**
3. **Maintained investment** (if investor)
4. **Apply for KITAP** (5-year permit)

<InfoCard variant="tip" title="KITAP Benefits for Investors">

- 5-year validity - Multiple entry - Easier extensions - More stability - Path
  to naturalization (optional)

</InfoCard>
