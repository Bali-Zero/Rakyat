## The Facts

TVRI reported that Ngurah Rai Immigration deported 18 foreigners between 9 and 13 November for different immigration violations. One was an Australian identified as NDM, who officials said had worked as a visa consultant at his wife's agency despite holding Investor ITAS. The report places his deportation on 12 November.

The total of 18 covers several types of cases. It does not mean 18 investors were deported, and this report does not establish a new blanket prohibition on all activities by investors.

---

## Why the actual role matters

Official visa classification distinguishes activities permitted under different categories. The E28A investor classification includes specified business and investment activities, and specified roles in the company in which the foreigner invests. It should not be read as unrestricted permission to perform any job at any company.

The public reporting does not supply the complete immigration file. It is therefore inappropriate to infer every detail of NDM's shareholding, sponsorship or employment arrangements.

---

## A practical review for an expat owner

Write down what you actually do during a working week. Include the company involved, your formal position, the services delivered and any customer-facing responsibilities. Compare that description with your current visa and stay-permit documents, and ask an appropriately qualified adviser about any mismatch.

For a PT PMA, keep the company role and the person's immigration documentation available for the same review. An informal description such as helping a family business does not explain the full arrangement.

---

## Keep the conclusion specific

The useful lesson is to check the real activity and the particular permission. Do not use a news case as a substitute for an individual assessment, or assume another person's visa outcome determines yours.
