# Tourist Visa Extension Indonesia 2026: B1 and C1 Guide

Planning to extend your stay in Indonesia? Check the visa index and the expiry date on your actual stay permit first. **B1 e-VOA allows an initial 30 days and one extension of up to 30 days: 60 days total.** An extension requires an application and approval; it is not automatic.

## Which Tourist Visas Can Be Extended?

| Entry route     | Initial stay               | Extension                                                 |
| --------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| B1 VOA / e-VOA  | Up to 30 days from arrival | Once, up to 30 more days; 60 days total                   |
| C1 tourism      | Up to 60 days from arrival | Extendable, up to 180 days total under its own conditions |
| Visa-free entry | Up to 30 days              | Not extendable                                            |

B1 and C1 are different visa categories. “B211A” is a legacy label in older guides: check the index on your issued visa rather than applying its old extension schedule. [B1 rules](https://jakartapusat.imigrasi.go.id/layanan/warga-negara-asing-wna/visa-republik-indonesia/b1-visa-saat-kedatangan-wisata) · [C1 rules](https://jakartapusat.imigrasi.go.id/layanan/warga-negara-asing-wna/visa-republik-indonesia/c1-visa-wisata) · [Visa-free entry](https://ngurahrai.imigrasi.go.id/Layanan%20WNA/)

## E-VOA Extension: Complete Process

1. Check your arrival date, permit expiry and whether you have already used your one B1 extension.
2. Open the [official e-Visa portal](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/) and follow its stay-permit extension instructions. Enter your actual accommodation address and postal code.
3. Upload the requested documents and follow the official payment instructions.
4. Monitor your account and email. Expect an immigration office visit for a biometric photo and short interview, even when an agent assists you.
5. Check the issued extension, identity details and new expiry date. Save your permit and receipts.

The appointment process must be reconfirmed with the responsible office before paying or handing over documents. Online submission does not mean a fully online extension. [Ngurah Rai procedure](https://ngurahrai.imigrasi.go.id/Layanan%20WNA/)

---

## C1 Extension: Different Process

**C1 is not an e-VOA extension.** The official C1 page describes a single-entry tourist visa with an initial stay of up to 60 days, extendable to a maximum of 180 days. Do not use the old “four 30-day extensions” formula.

Confirm the extension period, sponsor requirements and filing route for your actual C1 permit before paying or submitting documents. A multiple-entry visa is a separate category. [C1 conditions](https://jakartapusat.imigrasi.go.id/layanan/warga-negara-asing-wna/visa-republik-indonesia/c1-visa-wisata)

## Extension Timeline: What to Expect

Ngurah Rai publishes a filing deadline of **at least 7 days before the stay permit expires**. Reconfirm the applicable deadline and appointment availability with your responsible office before committing money or documents; do not assume every office uses identical arrangements. [Published deadline](https://ngurahrai.imigrasi.go.id/Layanan%20WNA/)

Processing and appointment timing depend on the office. Build in time for weekends, public holidays and corrections. Neither an agent nor a quoted processing estimate guarantees approval before a travel date.

---

## Documents Needed for Extension: Detailed Checklist

Prepare your passport, current visa/stay-permit details, return or onward ticket, and accurate accommodation address. Ngurah Rai lists a valid passport and onward/return ticket for VOA extensions. Follow the portal and office instructions for the exact uploads and originals. [Document requirements](https://ngurahrai.imigrasi.go.id/Layanan%20WNA/)

Do not treat a sponsor letter, a host’s identity card or printed photos as universal B1 requirements. C1 and other categories have their own requirements. Provide additional documents only when the applicable process requires them.

---

## Common Extension Mistakes (And How to Avoid Them)

- Applying for a second B1 extension: only one is available.
- Confusing visa validity for entry with the permitted length of stay: use the expiry on your actual stay permit.
- Selecting an office from an old address: confirm jurisdiction using your current accommodation.
- Assuming the agent replaces your attendance: plan for the photo and interview.
- Treating submission or payment as approval: check the official status and issued permit.
- Booking travel before confirming document availability and appointment dates.

---

## What to Do If Extension is Denied

Ask immigration for the reason and the lawful next steps. Check the expiry of your current permit immediately. A pending application, payment receipt, appeal or agent promise must not be treated as extra permission to stay.

Do not assume a refusal is rare, that another application will succeed, or that a fixed rush fee resolves the problem. If time is short, contact the responsible office promptly and arrange a lawful departure when required.

---

## When to Upgrade from Tourist Visa to Longer-Term Visa

If your plans exceed the B1 maximum, assess another appropriate visa before making commitments. C1 is for qualifying visits, not an extra B1 extension. B1 does not authorise employment or remote work. Work, investment and retirement routes have separate conditions.

A status change or bridging route may depend on your permit and eligibility. Do not assume every tourist must exit to change status, or that every visitor qualifies for an onshore change. Confirm your own route with immigration before booking travel or paying.

## Emergency Extension Options

If the published deadline has passed or your permit is close to expiry, contact the responsible immigration office immediately. Explain any emergency and keep supporting documents. There is no guaranteed last-minute approval or guaranteed border-run readmission.

Do not plan an overstay as a paid extension. A fine does not create permission to remain. Departure and any new admission are separate decisions; leaving consumes a single-entry visa.

## FAQs: Tourist Visa Extension Indonesia

### How many times can I extend B1 e-VOA?

Once, for up to 30 additional days after the initial 30 days, making a maximum of 60 days from arrival. Approval is required. [B1 rules](https://jakartapusat.imigrasi.go.id/layanan/warga-negara-asing-wna/visa-republik-indonesia/b1-visa-saat-kedatangan-wisata)

### Can I extend visa-free entry?

No. Visa-free entry is not the same as B1. It is not extendable. [Ngurah Rai guidance](https://ngurahrai.imigrasi.go.id/Layanan%20WNA/)

### Can I travel while my extension is pending?

Check appointment dates and whether immigration needs to retain your original passport before booking. Do not assume all domestic travel is prohibited, or that you can leave and preserve a single-entry permission.

### Does using an agent remove the office visit?

No such exemption should be assumed. Expect the photo and interview; reconfirm the current appointment procedure with the office before paying.

### Can a new visa or an appeal automatically extend my stay?

No. Verify your current lawful stay and the conditions of any proposed route with immigration. Do not count a pending request as an approved extension.
