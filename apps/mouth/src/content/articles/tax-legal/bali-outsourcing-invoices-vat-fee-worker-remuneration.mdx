## TL;DR

**The clarification concerns supplies of labor services that do not meet the VAT-exemption criteria.**

---

## The Facts

The clarification concerns supplies of labor services that do not meet the VAT-exemption criteria. For the relevant alternative tax base, the tax invoice must distinguish the service provider's charge from remuneration received by workers.

The official text of PMK 11/2025, amending Article 2 letter o of PMK 75/2010, specifies an alternative base of eleven-twelfths of the invoiced amount excluding worker remuneration when the stated conditions are met. Worker remuneration includes salaries, wages, honoraria, allowances and similar payments.

This is not a new tax imposed on every outsourcing arrangement, and it does not establish that every supplier can calculate VAT using only a line labelled management fee.

---

## What to ask your supplier

Our practical recommendation is to request the contract, commercial invoice and tax invoice together. Ask the supplier to explain how the components reconcile and the basis for the VAT treatment used.

Check whether the period, supplier identity and total match your records. If worker remuneration and the service charge are not separately identifiable, ask for clarification before treating the invoice as resolved.

---

## Keep tax and operational checks separate

A clear invoice is useful for accounting. It does not by itself prove that every labor, licensing or contractual requirement of the outsourcing arrangement has been met. Those questions need their own review.

Likewise, the existence of salaries in a bill does not determine the full VAT treatment without considering the relevant conditions and nature of the supply.

---

## For a Bali PT PMA

Give your accountant the supporting documents rather than only a screenshot of the total payable. Retain the supplier's written explanation with the payment record. If a correction is needed, reconcile it through the appropriate invoice process instead of silently changing figures in your own spreadsheet.

The aim is a payment file that explains both the commercial charge and the tax treatment applied to that specific arrangement.
