## The Facts

## A proposal, not a January payroll instruction
Indonesia is discussing a change to the non-taxable income allowance, known as PTKP, for 2027. On 5 November, Coordinating Economic Minister Airlangga Hartarto said the discussions were unfinished and the change would not take effect in January 2027, according to [ANTARA's report of his briefing](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5834307/airlangga-pastikan-penyesuaian-ptkp-belum-akan-berlaku-januari-2027).
His statement referred to possible implementation after January. It did not establish a final amount or a definite later commencement date. Bali employers should not translate that announcement into a confirmed payroll saving.
## Keep PTKP separate from other proposals
An [official Coordinating Ministry for Economic Affairs release](https://www.ekon.go.id/publikasi/detail/7124/dorong-pertumbuhan-2027-pemerintah-gulirkan-rangkaian-program-pelindungan-masyarakat-dan-stimulus-ekonomi) includes discussion of PTKP with the Finance Ministry among the government's planned economic measures. It also discusses other tax and worker-support measures.
Those policy strands should not be combined into a single automatic entitlement. PTKP concerns an individual's income-tax calculation; it is not a general reduction in a PT PMA's corporate income tax. A policy announcement also needs to be distinguished from its implementing rules.
## What a business can prepare now
As a planning exercise, keep a baseline payroll budget using the rules currently applicable to the relevant employee. Maintain any hypothetical future adjustment in a separate scenario, clearly labelled with its assumptions. Do not replace production payroll settings simply because a new allowance is under discussion.
Ask the payroll team to record the eventual regulation number, commencement date, eligible taxpayers and any transition instructions before applying a change. This helps separate a public announcement from an operational instruction.
## Expat employees need an individual check
Do not assume that every foreign employee receives the same treatment simply because they hold a stay permit or work for a PT PMA. Have the payroll adviser check the individual's tax position and the relevant withholding treatment before calculating an entitlement.
No final PTKP figure, universal expat benefit or guaranteed start date is asserted in this article. The useful action now is to monitor the official decision, keep employee records accurate and avoid promising a take-home-pay increase that has not been established.
