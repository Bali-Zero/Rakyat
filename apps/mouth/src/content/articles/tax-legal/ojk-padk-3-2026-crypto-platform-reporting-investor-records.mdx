## The Facts

## Start with the actual rule
[OJK's official regulation page](https://www.ojk.go.id/id/regulasi/Pages/PADK-Nomor-3-Tahun-2026-Penyelenggaraan-Perdagangan-Aset-Keuangan-Digital-termasuk-Aset-Kripto.aspx) identifies PADK 3/2026 as a regulation concerning digital financial asset trading, including crypto assets. It was established on 30 June 2026 and generally took effect on 1 September 2026.
Its subject includes reporting by trading operators, evaluation of assets and reporting procedures. The official summary identifies periodic and incidental reporting and specific transitional dates for parts of the previous circular. A blanket claim that every previous provision changed on the same day would miss those details.
## Why it is appearing in November
A [Legal 500 analysis published on 10 November](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/indonesia/technology/indonesias-new-regulation-on-crypto-and-digital-asset-transactions-key-highlights-of-ojk-board-member-regulation-no-3-of-2026-on-the-operation-of-digital-financial-asset-trading-including-crypto-assets) discusses the regulation's technical guidelines and reporting formats. The date of that analysis is not the rule's commencement date.
This article concerns an issued operator-reporting regulation. It should not be confused with a separate consultation, a proposed rule or an announcement about international tax-information exchange.
## A platform obligation is not automatically your filing obligation
The relevant question is who must submit which report. PADK 3/2026 addresses regulated market participants and their reporting arrangements. It does not, merely by existing, establish that every individual crypto holder must file the same operator reports.
For an expat investor in Bali, personal taxation and platform regulatory compliance are separate questions. This article does not determine an individual's residence status, calculate a tax liability or claim that every overseas wallet is automatically reported under this instrument.
## Keep usable transaction evidence
As a practical recordkeeping step, retain exchange statements and transaction histories while they remain accessible. Make a clear distinction between purchases, disposals and transfers between accounts you control. Record dates and fees, and keep supporting evidence for entries that need explanation.
These are organisational recommendations, not additional duties created by this article. Before responding to an account request, verify who is asking, which entity operates the platform and which specific requirement is being cited.
Operators should review the applicable reporting forms and transitional provisions against the official regulation. Individual investors should use their records for advice specific to their own situation, rather than treating a headline about platform reporting as a personal tax ruling.
