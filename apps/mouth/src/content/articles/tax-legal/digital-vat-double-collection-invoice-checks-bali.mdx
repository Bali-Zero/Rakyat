## The Facts

DJP has reiterated that the SPP-TDLN collection mechanism is not intended to collect Indonesian VAT twice on the same qualifying digital purchase. DDTC reported the clarification on 14 November 2026.

The official DJP notice states that transactions already subject to VAT collection by appointed PMSE collectors are outside SPP-TDLN collection. The two mechanisms operate alongside each other. This is a rule about the collection route, not a promise that every invoice or payment record will always be error-free.

PMK No. 49/2026 governs SPP-TDLN. Its legal commencement date and the operational rollout date are different: the JDIH record gives 20 July 2026, while DJP's notice identifies implementation from 25 September 2026.

---

## In Practice

A Bali business reviewing a software, advertising or cloud-service bill should compare three records: the supplier invoice, the payment confirmation and any separate tax-collection evidence.

Check whether the supplier has already identified Indonesian VAT. Then compare the billed total, currency, settlement date and bank debit. A difference alone does not prove double taxation: exchange-rate conversion or an unrelated payment fee may also need explanation.

If two entries appear to charge VAT for the same transaction, keep the transaction references and request a written breakdown from the supplier and payment provider. Ask which entity collected each amount and how a confirmed error should be corrected.
