## The Facts

A final income tax on derivatives is being discussed in Indonesia. The discussion should be followed as a policy proposal, not treated as a new charge already applying to every investor or PT PMA.

## What was discussed

Investortrust's 24 November 2026 report covers a seminar held in Jakarta on 23 November. Speakers discussed a final-tax approach for domestic commodity and financial derivatives, with collection potentially involving brokers and clearing houses.

The reported ideas included using transaction value or lot size and linking administration with Coretax. Those are design ideas described at the event. The report does not announce an enacted new rate, a commencement date, or final rules defining all affected taxpayers and instruments.

The discussion is most relevant to people and companies actually trading or using the affected products. Simply owning a PT PMA in Bali does not establish exposure to a derivatives-tax proposal.

## The old regulation is not a current template to apply

Government Regulation PP 17/2009 must not be described as a currently operative final-tax regime. The Ministry of Finance's official legal database records that PP 31/2011 repealed it, effective 6 June 2011.

That distinction matters when reading explanations that mix the old scheme with the new discussion. Historical rates and tax bases are not evidence of the terms of a future regulation.

## Questions to take to a broker or adviser

Investors can prepare a factual inventory without changing their tax treatment based on a headline:

- Which instruments are held, and through which exchange, broker or counterparty?
- Is each position personal or held by a company?
- Which documents record premiums, settlements, fees, gains and losses?
- What tax has actually been withheld, and what supporting evidence exists?
- Which jurisdiction and taxpayer status does the current advice assume?
- If a proposal becomes a regulation, which transactions and effective dates does its final text cover?

A transaction-based charge and a charge on net income can have different economic effects. Until the actual tax base and rate are fixed, calculating a reader's future liability would be speculative.

## What to watch next

Look for an officially issued regulation, the covered products and taxpayers, the collection mechanism, commencement provisions and any transition guidance. A seminar presentation, broker commentary or policy aspiration does not substitute for that text.

Do not assume that a future withholding mechanism will eliminate annual reporting, that all foreign investors will face one rate, or that treaty relief is automatic. Those questions require the final rules and the investor's actual circumstances.
