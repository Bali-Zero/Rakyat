## The Facts

DDTC quoted DJP's public-relations director Inge Diana Rismawanti as saying the homepage launch was planned for 23 November, with other pages updated progressively through the end of December.

At the time of this article, the launch is a plan. The report does not announce a new tax rate, a replacement tax identity or a requirement to move an account to an address supplied in an unsolicited message. A change to the public website's design should not be described as a new tax obligation.

---

## A simple preparation for your team

Our recommendation is to keep the official website address in your internal guidance. If your instructions rely on screenshots or menu positions, mark them for review after the new layout appears.

Make a short list of the pages staff regularly use and check whether the instructions still match. Avoid circulating a full replacement procedure until the actual interface can be viewed.

---

## Treat unexpected messages carefully

Navigate to the official site directly when checking an announcement. Do not treat a familiar logo, a message about an upgrade or a changed screenshot as proof that an unfamiliar link is official.

If access behaves differently, record the page address and the visible message without including passwords or one-time codes in a shared screenshot. Ask the appropriate support channel about the specific problem.
