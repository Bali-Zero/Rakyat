## The Facts

DJP expects to announce the first revenue figures from marketplace PPh Article 22 collections after November payment and reporting, according to DDTC's 15 November report quoting the tax authority. It reported that implementation by designated platforms began on 1 October 2026.

This is a reporting update. It should not be described as a tax created in November. The Finance Ministry's official register lists PMK 37/2025 as effective from 14 July 2025; the regulation date and a platform's operational start are different facts.

What PT PMA sellers should reconcile

DJP's official guidance describes a 0.5% collection on the relevant gross amount shown in the invoice, excluding VAT and luxury-goods sales tax. The collection is not automatically the seller's final income-tax liability. The treatment depends on the applicable taxpayer regime.

For the October sales cycle, compare platform invoices, sales records, returns and amounts collected with your accounting records. Ask your tax preparer how the collection should be recorded and, where applicable, credited.

Do not apply an individual seller's turnover exception to a corporate seller without checking the rules. The coming revenue announcement concerns aggregate collections; it will not by itself determine whether an individual business's filing is correct.
