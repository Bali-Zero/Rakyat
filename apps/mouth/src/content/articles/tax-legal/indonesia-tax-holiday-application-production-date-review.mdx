## The Facts

## A practical reason to reopen the incentive file

Companies using Indonesian investment incentives should be able to explain when they applied, what was approved and when commercial production began. Recent commentary on tax holidays makes that record especially useful, but it does not establish that every recipient has breached the rules.

[CITA's 19 October 2026 article](https://cita.or.id/bahaya-tax-holiday-salah-sasaran-ekonom-negara-bisa-rugi/) reports tax analyst Fajry Akbar's criticism of incentives reaching investors whose decisions or production were already underway. That is an analyst's assessment of policy targeting. His description of the feasibility-study stage should not replace the legal conditions attached to a particular facility.

## What the official audit statement establishes

In its [28 August 2026 statement](https://www.bpk.go.id/news/bpk-tekankan-pentingnya-tata-kelola-fasilitas-fiskal-penanaman-modal-yang-baik-kepada-kementerian-investasi-dan-hilirisasibkpm), BPK described a review covering fiscal facilities granted during 2021 through the first half of 2025. The scope included tax holidays, tax allowances and import-duty facilities.

BPK identified weaknesses needing improvement in verification and the certainty of application status. It recommended better data exchange and verification, while concluding that, except for the matters identified, the facilities had complied with the relevant rules in all material respects.

This is not an announcement of blanket cancellation. The public statement does not quantify a loss for an individual investor or decide the status of every approval.

## Read the source wording in context

The BPK statement itself qualifies its conclusion with “kecuali hal-hal yang telah dijelaskan di atas” and ends with “dalam semua hal yang material”. These mean that the stated exceptions and the material-compliance conclusion must be read together.

CITA quotes Fajry: “Fasilitas ini bukan untuk investor yang telah memutuskan berinvestasi di Indonesia, apalagi jika sudah berproduksi.” That is the analyst's reported view about targeting, not the text of a new eligibility rule.

Both linked pages were opened for this review on 20 October 2026. BPK's page displays 28 August 2026; CITA's page displays 19 October 2026. The original Indonesian passages let readers distinguish the audit conclusion from the later commentary.

## Build one chronology from original records

Our practical recommendation is to assemble a dated chronology before drawing conclusions from the headline. Start with the submitted application and acknowledgement, the approval and its conditions, amendments or official correspondence, and the records used to establish production milestones.

Keep application, approval, trial activity and commercial production as separate entries. Where two records disagree, retain both and ask the adviser responsible for the facility to determine which event matters under the applicable rule. Do not invent a convenient date to make the file appear consistent.

A reviewer should be able to move from each date in the chronology to the underlying document. Record the document owner and where the original is kept so the company can answer a follow-up without rebuilding its history from email.

## Check exposure before changing tax treatment

CITA discusses possible withdrawal and repayment consequences where the applicable requirements are not met. It does not prove that those consequences have been imposed on your company.

If the chronology raises a question, obtain a facility-specific review of the governing provisions, approval conditions and any official notice. A general audit headline is not enough to calculate a repayment or amend a tax return.

For a Bali PT PMA that has never received or applied for the relevant incentive, this story does not create a new tax-holiday filing obligation. Establish the company's actual position first.

## Bali Zero take

A complete approval file is more useful than an alarming headline. Give the person reviewing the incentive the chronology, original approval and unresolved discrepancies together, and request a documented conclusion about any next step.
