## The Facts

## A collection target, not a new tax bill

Indonesia's tax authority still needed to collect roughly Rp500 trillion against its 2026 revenue target, according to reports of Director General of Taxes Bimo Wijayanto's remarks in Bogor on 12 November 2026.

Warta Ekonomi's report gives the annual tax-revenue target as Rp2,357.7 trillion. The approximately Rp500 trillion figure referred to the amount still to be pursued during the remaining period of the year.

The figure was reported as preliminary. The same coverage said fuller fiscal information was expected in the subsequent APBN KiTa briefing. Readers should not treat the remarks as a final audited year-end result.

Most importantly, the reported shortfall is a national collection figure. It is not an amount newly billed to expats, a new PT PMA tax rate or a penalty automatically imposed on businesses.

## What the announcement does and does not establish

A revenue target describes what the government aims to collect. It does not, by itself, change the legal basis of an individual taxpayer's liability.

The reporting reviewed for this article does not announce a new rate, an automatic assessment for foreign-owned companies or a mass-audit order. Those claims would require separate official evidence.

This distinction matters when a large number is shared without context. A headline about collections can easily be misread as a new obligation when no such measure has been announced in the cited remarks.

## Practical preparation for Bali businesses

A useful response is a focused records check, rather than treating the headline as a demand for an extra payment.

Ask the finance team whether payment references can be matched to the relevant returns and transactions, whether any unresolved differences have been recorded, and whether official correspondence is being handled by the authorised person.

These are organisational questions. They do not imply that a business has underpaid tax or needs to amend a return.

For a specific notice or disputed amount, assess the actual document and the applicable rules. Do not infer the outcome from a national revenue target.

## What to watch next

Look for the official fiscal release to confirm the latest reporting period, collection total and comparison with the target. Any later tax measure should be assessed on its own published terms and effective date.

This article explains the 12 November statement. It does not present an unverified later figure as an update.
