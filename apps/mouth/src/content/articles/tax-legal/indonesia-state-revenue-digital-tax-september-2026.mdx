## TL;DR

Reported Q3 state revenue grew 25.5%. Digital tax figures use separate periods: about IDR12.72 trillion for January–September 2026 and IDR58.75 trillion cumulatively through September.

---

## The Facts

Indonesia's state revenue and grants reached IDR2,341.5 trillion through the third quarter of 2026, growing 25.5% year on year, according to Finance Minister Suahasil Nazara's remarks at the 3 November KSSK press conference as reported by detikFinance. The growth figure refers to state revenue and grants, rather than tax receipts alone.

The same report puts state expenditure at IDR2,660.5 trillion. Those two amounts imply a gap of IDR319 trillion. Financing of IDR462 trillion is a different accounting measure and should not be presented as the deficit itself.

Digital-economy tax collection has a separate reporting basis. Taxnesia's 9 November summary, linking to DJP reporting carried by Kontan and CNN Indonesia, gives approximately IDR12.72 trillion for January–September 2026 and IDR58.75 trillion cumulatively through September. The latter is not nine months of 2026 receipts.

The summary lists cumulative PMSE VAT of IDR45.21 trillion, government-procurement platform taxes of IDR5.96 trillion, fintech taxes of IDR5.40 trillion and crypto taxes of IDR2.18 trillion. Those categories total IDR58.75 trillion. It separately identifies IDR9.54 trillion of PMSE VAT for January–September 2026.

DJP's earlier official August release illustrates the same distinction: cumulative digital-economy collections of IDR57.23 trillion included PMSE VAT receipts collected over several years.

---

## In Practice

A collection total does not change a business's tax rate or create a registration obligation by itself. PMSE VAT collector status depends on the relevant rules and appointment. Selling online is not sufficient reason to assume every seller must immediately register as an appointed collector.

Likewise, withholding shown by a payment platform should be reconciled with the taxpayer's own records and applicable annual reporting. Different digital tax categories should not be treated as one interchangeable charge.

## Bali Zero Take

### Our Analysis

The practical value of these figures is the reminder to keep digital purchase invoices, platform statements and tax records consistent. Revenue growth alone does not prove that a particular Bali business faces an audit or that a new tax has been introduced.

For budgeting, use the tax treatment of the actual transaction. Do not use a cumulative national collection figure as a forecast for the company's liability.
