## The Facts

# DJP's Five Year-End Tax Priorities: What Bali Businesses Should Monitor

Indonesia's tax authority has outlined five programmes for the closing months of 2026. For business owners in Bali, the practical response is to identify the programmes relevant to their own transactions.

## What DJP said

JawaPos reported on 17 November that DJP spokesperson Inge Diana had described the following priorities in comments made on 16 November:

1. Reducing or removing administrative sanctions.
2. Implementing the global minimum tax.
3. Implementing Article 22 income-tax collection through marketplaces.
4. Optimising VAT collection on overseas digital transactions through SPP-TDLN.
5. Monitoring periodic payments and substantive taxpayer compliance.

The report also described attention to major revenue-contributing sectors and the digital economy. These are attributed statements about DJP's agenda. The list itself is not a new regulation establishing identical obligations for every taxpayer.

## Separate the programme from your eligibility

An announcement about sanction relief is not evidence that a particular penalty has been cancelled. Ask which instrument, application requirements and decision apply to your own case.

Similarly, foreign ownership alone does not establish that a PT PMA falls within the global minimum tax. PMK 136/2024 defines a multinational-group framework; group structure and the applicable scope need to be assessed.

For marketplace and overseas digital transactions, identify the actual seller, platform, supplier, invoice and collection mechanism. A headline is not enough to determine whether a particular payment has already been taxed correctly.

## A focused year-end review

A useful internal review can bring together the sales ledger, platform statements, supplier invoices, payment evidence and filed returns for the relevant periods. List any unexplained differences before deciding whether a correction is necessary.

Assign one person to collect the records and another to confirm the applicable treatment with the company's adviser. Keep the analysis transaction-specific.

This checklist is an editorial preparation suggestion. The reported agenda does not establish that every Bali business will face an audit, receive relief or owe an additional tax.
