## The Facts

Indonesia's reported IDR 60.1 trillion in digital-economy tax collections is a revenue statistic. It is not a new tax rate, a bill for individual businesses, or evidence that every foreign resident has the same tax obligations.

## Read the period before reading the headline

Ideatax's 23 November 2026 report attributes the figure to the Directorate General of Taxes, with a cutoff of 31 October 2026. It describes cumulative receipts across electronic commerce, crypto transactions, financial technology and government procurement.

PMSE VAT accounts for a reported IDR 46.36 trillion accumulated since collection began in 2020. The separate January–October 2026 PMSE VAT figure is IDR 10.69 trillion. Confusing the cumulative and annual periods materially changes the meaning of the headline.

The report says 280 businesses had been appointed as PMSE VAT collectors, of which 249 had collected and remitted VAT. These figures describe the collector system, not the number of Bali businesses paying digital service invoices.

The underlying DJP release was not directly retrieved for this article. These statistics are therefore attributed to the published reporting, rather than presented as independently audited figures.

## What matters on a business invoice

For a Bali company buying online advertising, software or other digital services, a useful starting point is its own records. The national total does not determine the treatment of a specific purchase.

A practical monthly review can bring together:

- The supplier's identity and the business account used to place the order.
- The invoice date, service period, currency and description.
- Any tax shown separately by the supplier.
- Proof of payment and the amount recorded in the company's accounts.
- Refunds, credits, cancellations and recurring renewals.
- The documents an accountant needs to assess the applicable treatment.

Do not assume that every tax line is recoverable, that an overseas supplier means no Indonesian tax, or that two amounts on a statement represent duplicate tax. Each conclusion needs the relevant transaction documents and rules.

## Keep the statistic separate from compliance advice

This news does not announce a new rate, a new filing deadline or an automatic audit of PT PMA owners. Nor does it establish that the absence of new collector appointments means a change in enforcement policy.

If an invoice looks inconsistent, ask the supplier for clarification and have the company's accountant reconcile it against the transaction. Preserve the original document and any correction, so the record explains what changed.
