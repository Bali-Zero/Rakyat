## The Facts

## A cumulative total, not one year's receipts

Indonesia's Directorate General of Taxes reported Rp2.18 trillion in crypto-asset tax receipts through 30 September 2026. Its 31 October release shows that this total combines collections from 2022 onward. The January–September 2026 component was Rp300.28 billion. [DJP official release](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/siaran-pers/pajak-ekonomi-digital-tembus-rp5875-triliun-hingga-september-2026)

DJP breaks the cumulative crypto total into Rp1.30 trillion of Article 22 income tax and Rp881.82 billion of domestic VAT. Historical collections do not, by themselves, identify the tax treatment of a new transaction.

For Bali readers following crypto news, this distinction matters before using a headline to draw conclusions about current rules or an individual's liability.

## Keep the reporting periods separate

A multi-year total and a partial-year figure answer different questions. Comparing them without their dates can create the impression of growth or decline that the comparison does not establish.

The same DJP release places crypto within a wider cumulative digital-economy tax total of Rp58.75 trillion. It also records September appointments of Namecheap and two Airwallex entities as PMSE VAT collectors. Those appointments should not be described as evidence that every bank transfer or all cross-border financial flows automatically became taxable or visible to DJP. [DJP release and collection categories](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/siaran-pers/pajak-ekonomi-digital-tembus-rp5875-triliun-hingga-september-2026)

## A practical record review for crypto users

The following are record-keeping suggestions, not a calculation of tax due.

Keep exports of the transaction history from each platform used, rather than relying only on a current balance screenshot. Retain dates, asset quantities, transaction identifiers, fees and any tax amounts shown in the platform's records.

Organise records so that a reviewer can distinguish trades, deposits, withdrawals and transfers between accounts. If the same movement appears in more than one platform export, flag the matching records so it is not accidentally treated as two unrelated events.

Where a transaction relates to a business, keep the supporting agreement or invoice with the payment evidence. A clear explanation of purpose is more useful than assuming that all crypto movements represent the same kind of income.

## What to ask before drawing a tax conclusion

Give the adviser the transaction dates, platforms involved, ownership of the accounts and the underlying purpose of each activity. Ask which current provisions apply to the particular facts and how any platform-collected tax should be reconciled.

Being an expat in Bali or a PT PMA shareholder does not answer those questions on its own. This revenue announcement is not an individual tax assessment, a new rate announcement or proof that a mass audit will follow.

The immediate takeaway is to label the headline correctly and maintain records that can support a transaction-by-transaction review.
