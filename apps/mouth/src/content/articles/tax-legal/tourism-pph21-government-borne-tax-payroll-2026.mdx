## TL;DR

**PMK 105/2025 provides the 2026 government-borne PPh 21 framework for qualifying employees of specified employers, including listed tourism activities.**

---

## The Facts

PMK 105/2025 provides the 2026 government-borne PPh 21 framework for qualifying employees of specified employers, including listed tourism activities. The regulation requires an eligible main KLU in the tax administration’s records; an informal description of the business is not enough.

For qualifying permanent employees, Article 4 uses fixed and regular gross income of no more than Rp10 million in January 2026, or the first month of employment for someone starting during 2026. It also includes tax-identity and non-duplication conditions. Non-permanent workers have separate income tests.

DDTC’s 20 November coverage refers to a target of 552,000 tourism workers. That target must not be reported as proof that all of them have already received the benefit.

---

## In Practice

Prepare an eligibility worksheet for each employee, with the employer’s recorded main KLU, the relevant starting-month pay, tax identity and any other government-borne PPh 21 benefit. Ask payroll to retain the supporting records and confirm the applicable reporting procedure.

The regulation links the benefit to payment to the employee and periodic reporting. Keep the payroll calculation, payment evidence and return consistent. Do not simply remove withholding across the entire workforce because the company operates a hotel or restaurant.

For foreign staff, review the individual tax position and all programme conditions rather than assuming that either nationality or a KITAS alone settles eligibility.

---

## What This Does Not Establish

This is a summary of the programme, not an eligibility determination for a specific employer or worker. The regulation should be read in full before claiming relief or correcting payroll.
