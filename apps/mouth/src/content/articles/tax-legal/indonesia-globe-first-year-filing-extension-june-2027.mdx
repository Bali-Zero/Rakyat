**Indonesia's first GloBE annual income-tax return can be extended by up to two months through an extension notification.**

## The Facts

Indonesia's first GloBE annual income-tax return can be extended by up to two months through an extension notification. DJP's official compliance guidance describes a conditional filing extension, rather than an automatic blanket postponement.

For the calendar-year example discussed by DJP official Ririn Wahyuni Nasrul and reported by Ortax, the 2025 GloBE imposition year is reported through the 2026 GloBE tax year. Payment falls due on 31 December 2026. The ordinary annual-return deadline is 30 April 2027, with a first-year extension potentially taking filing to 30 June 2027.

These dates should not be applied indiscriminately to groups with different financial year ends. DJP states that top-up tax is payable in the following GloBE tax year, by the end of that year.

The regime concerns qualifying multinational groups, generally subject to the EUR750 million consolidated-revenue test in at least two of the preceding four fiscal years. Indonesia's framework targets a minimum effective rate of 15%, with detailed calculations, exclusions and reliefs determining the result. PMA status alone does not establish that a company is in scope.

---

## In Practice

The annual return, GloBE Information Return (GIR), notification and tax payment are separate compliance items. DJP gives the GIR and related notification a first-year deadline of 18 months after the relevant imposition year ends, compared with 15 months ordinarily. A filing extension for the annual return should not be assumed to extend payment or another filing.

An effective rate above 15% also does not establish that all administrative obligations disappear. The group's Indonesian entities should review their reporting roles and the availability of any applicable safe harbour.

### Our Analysis

The useful planning question is whether the group has a documented compliance calendar for each obligation. A headline saying June 2027 can obscure the payment date and the notification required to obtain the annual-return extension.
