## The Facts


Discussion about redesigning investment incentives is relevant to international investors, but a policy proposal is not a benefit a company can already claim.

## What is being debated

A CITA publication dated 16 November discusses the consequences of global tax commitments for Indonesia's investment incentives. It presents policy arguments, including proposals associated with substance-based incentives and qualified refundable tax credits.

The article is commentary on policy options. It does not establish that Indonesia has enacted a new credit, grant or automatic replacement for an existing incentive.

## The existing tax framework is separate

Indonesia's global minimum tax framework is set out in PMK 136/2024. Its scope includes a consolidated-revenue test of at least EUR750 million in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years for relevant multinational groups, with specific rules and exceptions.

A PT PMA's foreign ownership therefore does not, by itself, establish that it is in scope. The relevant assessment concerns the group and the regulation's detailed conditions, rather than merely the local company's label.

The regulation and the debate about future investment support should be read as separate subjects. A proposal cannot be substituted for an enacted provision or a company's actual approval.

## What investors can prepare

Keep a schedule of incentives already granted to the company, including the approval document, conditions and period. Separate those from proposals mentioned in presentations or news reports.

For a business belonging to a multinational group, ask group finance to confirm whether the minimum-tax framework applies and who is responsible for the assessment.

When comparing expansion options, model the current approved position first. Any possible future incentive can be shown as a separate scenario with an explicit assumption, not as guaranteed cash flow.

These are practical planning suggestions. This article does not determine a company's tax liability or confirm eligibility for an incentive.
