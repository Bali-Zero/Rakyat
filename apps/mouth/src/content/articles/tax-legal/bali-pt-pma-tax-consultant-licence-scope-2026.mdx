## The Facts


A competence certificate and a practising licence answer different questions. For a foreign-owned company in Bali, checking both is a useful first step before appointing a tax consultant.

## The rule behind the distinction

The Ministry of Finance's JDIH records PMK 55/2026 on tax consultants and other authorised taxpayer representatives as effective from 24 August 2026. This is an explanation of an existing regulation, not a new November requirement.

The ministry's training agency, BPPK, explains that a Certificate of Competence, or SKK, alone does not authorise someone to practise as a tax consultant. The person must satisfy the other requirements and obtain a tax consultant licence.

Its official FAQ distinguishes licence levels A, B and C. Level B excludes foreign-investment entities and permanent establishments, among other exclusions; level C covers all individual and corporate taxpayers. This makes licence scope particularly relevant when appointing a consultant for a PT PMA.

## What a Bali business can check

Before signing an engagement, ask for the consultant's current licence and classification, the identity of the professional responsible for the work, and a written description of the assignment.

Keep company work separate from the owner's personal tax affairs in the engagement letter. A company return, an individual's return and representation in a particular matter are different assignments. The appropriate scope should be checked for each.

For an expatriate individual, nationality alone should not replace an assessment of the person's actual taxpayer circumstances. The official classifications include distinctions involving domicile in tax-treaty partner countries.

## A practical appointment checklist

Record who will prepare documents, who reviews them, who submits them and what evidence of submission you will receive. Agree on deadlines, access arrangements and how queries will be handled.

These are practical procurement checks, not a finding that an existing adviser has acted improperly. The new framework should not be read as automatically invalidating every older licence or filing.
