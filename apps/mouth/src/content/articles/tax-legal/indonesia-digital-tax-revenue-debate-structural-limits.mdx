## The Facts

An analysis by Tenggara Strategics, republished by Windonesia on 16 November 2026, argues that expanded digital-tax collection alone cannot resolve Indonesia's wider fiscal challenges. The underlying Jakarta Post commentary appeared earlier in November.

This is economic analysis, not a new tax regulation. Its argument about the tax base should not be presented as an enacted change in a Bali business's tax rate.

There is also a separate measurement issue. DDTC reported on 14 November that DJP was still reconciling SPP-TDLN transaction data and did not yet have a confirmed revenue figure to announce. The report said an estimate was expected later in November. An expectation of a future report is not the report itself.

---

## In Practice

When reading a digital-tax headline, distinguish three things: a collection mechanism, transactions processed, and revenue actually collected. A large transaction count does not establish the tax yield, and a forecast does not establish cash already received.

For a PT PMA, these distinctions help keep budgeting grounded. Review the bills and payment evidence relevant to the business rather than adding a speculative tax surcharge simply because national fiscal debate has intensified.
