## The Facts

## A deduction is not necessarily the final calculation
[NusaBali reported on 2 November](https://www.nusabali.com/berita/235178/pajak-dipotong-platform-kreator-tetap-wajib-hitung-pph) a reminder to content creators that platform deductions do not remove the need to calculate their annual income tax. The report concerns a statement made on 1 November.
The important distinction is between money withheld during the year and the calculation that applies to the taxpayer's full position. Do not assume that a lower platform payout proves every tax obligation has been settled.
## Read the example within its scope
An [educational article hosted by DJP](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/artikel/affiliator-cuan-jalan-pajak-aman), published on 18 June, explains an individual affiliate-income example in which PPh 21 withheld by a platform is non-final and can be credited in the annual calculation with supporting withholding evidence.
That article carries an express disclaimer that it represents its author's personal opinion. It is useful explanatory material, not an individual ruling for every creator, foreign resident or company.
Its examples should not be applied automatically to a PT PMA receiving revenue in its own name. Before deciding how a deduction is treated, establish who earned the income, what service or transaction generated it and which tax was actually withheld.
## Build a record that can be reconciled
For each platform, keep the statement period, gross amount, platform fees, tax deduction and net payout in separate fields. Match the payout to the bank receipt without losing the explanation for the difference.
A simple editorial worksheet can include the platform's transaction reference and a link to its withholding document. Mark missing evidence as missing; do not create an estimated certificate or treat a fee labelled commission as tax without checking.
This approach also helps avoid counting the same income twice when a dashboard statement and a bank deposit describe the same transaction.
## Expat creators need a personal assessment
For someone living in Bali, the location of a platform or bank account does not by itself settle the person's complete tax position. Have the relevant residence, income and withholding facts reviewed before assuming a credit or exemption is available.
The practical next step is document collection and reconciliation. A platform deduction can be part of the answer, but the evidence must show what it represents and how it belongs in the applicable annual calculation.
