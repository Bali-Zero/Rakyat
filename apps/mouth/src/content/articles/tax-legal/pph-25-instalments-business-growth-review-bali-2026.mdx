## The Facts

The relevant starting point is Article 120 of PER-11/PJ/2025. It provides for recalculating remaining-year PPh 25 instalments where business activity has increased and estimated annual income tax exceeds 125% of the tax used as the basis for the existing instalments. The comparison concerns income tax, not simply a 25% increase in sales.

The DJP legal text also provides a separate reduction application process in Article 119. These mechanisms should not be confused with a universal increase for all companies. A November consultant commentary has brought instalment adjustments back into discussion; the underlying rule is not new this month.

---

## In Practice

For a Bali PT PMA, prepare the previous calculation basis, tax paid so far, year-to-date accounts and a documented forecast for the remaining months. Ask the tax adviser to distinguish recurring operating income from one-off items and explain the projected annual tax calculation.

A simplified illustration: if the relevant tax basis were Rp100 million, 125% would be Rp125 million. That illustrates the comparison only; it is not a complete tax computation or a payment instruction. The company must still establish the applicable basis and remaining instalments.

Keep a dated calculation and the reason for any revision. A tourism business with seasonal revenue should avoid treating one busy month as a complete annual forecast.

---

## What This Does Not Establish

The 125% test is not a new corporate tax rate. This article does not determine a particular company’s instalment, entitlement to a reduction or treatment under other tax regimes. Review the applicable rules and company records before changing payments.
