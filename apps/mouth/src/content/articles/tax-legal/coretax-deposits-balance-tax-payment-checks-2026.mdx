## The Facts

# Coretax Deposits Fall: Why a Stored Balance Is Different from a Tax Payment

A lower national tax-deposit figure does not tell you whether your own company's tax has been paid correctly. For a Bali PT PMA, the useful distinction is between money available in a deposit and evidence that a particular obligation has been settled.

## What the reported decline means

IKPI reported on 15 November that tax deposits through 31 October 2026 stood at Rp33.6 trillion, down 49.8% year on year. It attributed the change to the finance minister's explanation that more payments were being directed to the appropriate revenue categories instead of remaining in deposit accounts.

Those aggregate figures should not be treated as a diagnosis of an individual taxpayer's account. They do not prove that a particular company has an unpaid bill or an available refund.

## What the official Coretax guide shows

DJP's Coretaxpedia guidance, updated on 1 October 2025, describes using a deposit to pay a tax bill or an underpaid return. The payment process involves selecting the deposit option and having sufficient funds. The guide also explains where payment documents and a successfully filed return can be checked.

The practical point is that a deposit balance and a completed payment or filing record are different pieces of evidence.

## What to reconcile

For each relevant tax period, place these records side by side:

- The bank payment or funding evidence.
- The deposit movement and allocation record.
- The tax bill or return to which the funds were applied.
- The payment document and filing receipt, where applicable.

If the records disagree, identify the specific transaction and period before deciding on the next action. Keep screenshots and reference numbers for any query raised through official support channels.

Do not make a second payment or amend a return solely because a news headline says deposits have fallen. A reconciliation should establish what happened in the company's own account first.
