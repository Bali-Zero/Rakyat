## The Facts

DJP has confirmed an official email campaign concerning withholding slips removed from 2025 individual income-tax returns. Its announcement PENG-62/PJ.09/2026, dated 10 November, asks affected taxpayers to check their income and withholding records and correct a return if it does not reflect the facts.

For an expatriate with an Indonesian personal filing, the first step is a reconciliation—not an assumption that the email itself proves an unpaid liability.

Check the message and the documents

DJP identifies @pajak.go.id as its official email domain. The notice also says it does not request payments to personal accounts, passwords, one-time passcodes or screen sharing. Confirm doubtful messages through the tax office or a service channel independently accessed from pajak.go.id.

Gather the submitted return, income statements, withholding documents and relevant payment records. Compare the tax year, taxpayer details and amounts. Record why a document was omitted, whether it was duplicated, and whether the reported income is complete. This working file makes a specific question easier to resolve with a tax preparer.

Keep sanction relief separate

The email announcement is not a promise of automatic sanction relief. A separate DJP release on PMK 71/2026 explains that relief remains conditional and does not erase the underlying tax obligation. Check the applicable process before relying on a relief deadline reported elsewhere.

The campaign concerns individual returns for 2025; it should not be presented as a blanket finding against all foreign residents or as a corporate PT PMA filing notice.
