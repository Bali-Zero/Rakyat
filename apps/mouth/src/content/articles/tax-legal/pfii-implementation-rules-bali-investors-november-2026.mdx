## The Facts

Indonesia's Finance Ministry is preparing four implementing instruments for the Pusat Finansial Internasional Indonesia, or PFII, according to DDTC's 14 November report. It cites remarks by Herman Saheruddin at the previous day's APBN Kita briefing.

The reported package consists of a presidential regulation, a government regulation and two ministerial regulations. It covers institutional arrangements, designation procedures, tax facilities and funding. The development is work on implementation, not confirmation that every proposed benefit is already available to every investor.

What this means for a Bali PT PMA

The report does not establish that an ordinary Bali company qualifies for PFII treatment or that a particular Bali location has been designated. Foreign ownership alone is not evidence of eligibility under a scheme whose detailed arrangements still need checking.

Before putting an incentive into a financial model, identify the published implementing instrument, commencement provisions, eligible activity and applicant requirements. Separate a proposal discussed in the news from a designation or approval that applies to a specific entity.

The next useful update is the official release of those instruments. Until their texts can be reviewed, this article makes no promise of a particular exemption, rate or application route. Investors should revisit their assumptions when the official implementation texts become available.
