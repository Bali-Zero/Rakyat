## The Facts

## A new option on an existing corridor

Bali's Transportation Agency announced the start of electric Trans Sarbagita bus operations on 9 November 2026. The trial serves Corridor I, linking GOR Ngurah Rai in Denpasar with Garuda Wisnu Kencana, or GWK.

The agency described a fleet of 11 electric buses: ten for daily operation and one reserve. DAMRI operates the service under the provincial transport programme.

The trial is free, but passengers are asked to tap an electronic-money card so journeys can be recorded. ANTARA's announcement ahead of the launch also described assistance for passengers without a card.

These are trial arrangements. They should not be presented as a permanent fare policy or a guarantee that every future journey will be free.

## What this means for residents and visitors

The practical benefit is another public-transport option along the Denpasar–GWK corridor. It may help a traveller whose starting point and destination fit that route.

It is not an announcement of an airport rail link or an airport shuttle. “Ngurah Rai” in the starting point refers to the named sports venue in Denpasar; readers should not confuse it with the airport.

A bus route also does not answer the entire door-to-door journey. The distance to a stop, an onward connection and the timing of the return trip can determine whether it works for a particular day.

## Before trying the service

Check the current operating information with the transport agency or operator. Confirm the boarding location, direction and last practical return option. Bring an electronic-money card if available and follow staff instructions about recording the trip.

For accommodation operators, a helpful guest note would describe the corridor and explain how guests can verify current information. Avoid promising an exact journey time or departure frequency unless it has been checked for that service.

## What has not been established here

This article does not provide a verified full timetable, every stop, permanent fare rules or a guaranteed journey time. Those details can change during a trial.

The confirmed development is the launch of electric operations on the specified corridor. Plan around the published route and current operator information, rather than treating the launch as an island-wide service.
