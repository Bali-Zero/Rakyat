## The Facts

A local electric-shuttle promotion offers another way to explore part of Sanur this November. ANTARA reported on 14 November that the service in Desa Adat Intaran is introducing QRIS and QRIS Tap payments.

The reported standard fare is Rp5,000 per person. A promotional Rp68 fare is available from 15 to 30 November 2026, subject to a daily quota. The quota's size is not specified in the report.

According to the village enterprise representative quoted by ANTARA, the service has 12 vehicles, with capacity for up to ten passengers each. It serves destinations around Intaran rather than providing an island-wide transport network.

Before planning a trip

Confirm the stop, operating hours, available seats and remaining promotional allocation with the operator. Check that your payment method supports the required transaction; the announcement does not establish compatibility with every foreign wallet or bank card.

Allow a backup transport option if the promotional allocation is exhausted. The Rp68 figure is a temporary offer, not a permanent replacement for the stated normal fare.

For Sanur residents and visitors, the immediate benefit is a local mobility option. It should not be confused with a separate bus route or presented as a guaranteed airport connection.
