## The Facts

Bali Post reported on 22 November 2026 that Festival Ekraf Mangucita is scheduled for 27–29 November at Lapangan Mangupraja Mandala in the Badung Government Centre. The announced performers include D’Masiv, Perunggu, Barasuara, Ari Kencana and Lolot.

The report quotes Badung Tourism Office head Anak Agung Putri Mas Agung on preparations involving security, traffic, health services and the layout of activities. It also describes coordination with MSME participants on booth operations and waste management. These are announced plans, not a report that the festival has already taken place.

For expat residents, the event offers a possible way to discover local creative businesses and music outside a usual tourism itinerary. Before travelling, verify the daily programme, entry arrangements, location pin and any changes through the organiser’s current announcements.
