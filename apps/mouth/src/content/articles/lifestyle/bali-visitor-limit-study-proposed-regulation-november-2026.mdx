## The Facts

An official Bali Provincial Government release dated 20 November 2026 reports Governor Wayan Koster’s remarks at Udayana University’s Senastek event in Jimbaran on 19 November. He called for applied research to support Bali’s development, including a study of the island’s carrying capacity and plans for a regional regulation addressing foreign visitor numbers.

The release describes a policy direction and research agenda. It does not announce a numerical entry cap, a booking allocation system or a start date for an enforceable quota.

Continue checking official immigration and travel information for requirements that actually apply to their trip.

## What This Does Not Establish

The announcement is not a current entry ban and does not establish that existing visas or residence permissions have been cancelled. Any later regulation will need separate review for its scope, commencement and implementation.
