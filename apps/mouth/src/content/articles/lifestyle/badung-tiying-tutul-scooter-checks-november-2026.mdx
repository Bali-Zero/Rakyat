## The Facts

Balipuspanews reported on 18 November 2026 that Badung traffic police conducted an operation at Simpang Tiying Tutul, Mengwi, on 17 November. The report counted 41 riders: eight received tickets and 33 received warnings.

It attributed six ticketed cases to noisy exhausts and two to number-plate issues. Five of those ticketed and 22 of those warned were foreign nationals. These are the results of one operation, not an island-wide estimate of foreign riders’ behaviour. The report attributed the enforcement explanation to Badung traffic police chief AKP Ni Luh Sutari Ani.

---

## In Practice

Before accepting a rental scooter, inspect it in daylight. Check the helmet’s fit and fastening, visible number plates, lights, mirrors, brakes and tyres. Ask about any aftermarket exhaust and document the vehicle’s condition with the rental provider. These are practical safety checks, not a guarantee that the vehicle meets every legal requirement.

Confirm that your driving entitlement covers the motorcycle you intend to ride and that you have the appropriate vehicle documentation. Do not assume the rental shop’s willingness to hand over a key settles either issue. If uncertain, use another transport option while checking.

Keep the rental provider’s contact details and the agreed assistance process accessible. If stopped, cooperate with officers and use the official process for any enforcement action.
