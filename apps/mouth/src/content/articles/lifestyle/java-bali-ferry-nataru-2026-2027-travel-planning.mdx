## The Facts

Banyuwangi’s official government news service reported on 21 November 2026 that agencies were coordinating preparations for Christmas 2026 and New Year 2027 traffic on the Ketapang–Gilimanuk crossing. The report describes a planned delaying system and layered controls on the approach to Banyuwangi, potentially beginning as far away as Probolinggo.

This is advance traffic-management planning. It does not mean the ports are closed or that every journey will be delayed for a fixed number of hours.

---

## In Practice

For an expat family driving between Java and Bali, avoid treating the ferry leg as a guaranteed short connection between tightly timed bookings. Check the operator’s current booking process, vehicle details, sailing information and official traffic notices close to departure.

Prepare for waiting outside the immediate port area as well as inside it. Carry the essentials your passengers need, keep navigation and contact information available, and allow for changes caused by weather or traffic management.

Tourism businesses arranging transfers should explain which parts of the journey are estimates. A hotel check-in or onward flight should not depend on an assumed crossing time that has not been confirmed.
