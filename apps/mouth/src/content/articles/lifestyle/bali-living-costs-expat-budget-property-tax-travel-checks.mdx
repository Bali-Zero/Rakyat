## The Facts

A living-cost checklist is only useful if its dates and assumptions are clear. A commercial article circulated in October 2026 still describes Bali costs for 2024. Its publication date does not make the older figures current. This guide therefore does not reuse its property-tax calculation, accountant-price range or flight fares as verified 2026 prices.

For a dated official reference, Bali's September 2026 consumer-price figures show inflation of 0.32% month on month and 3.79% year on year. The figures come from the BPS release of 1 October and are reproduced in a Bank Indonesia Bali statement on the provincial government's website. [Read the official provincial publication](https://bmc.baliprov.go.id/news/title/inflasi-bali-september-2026-melandai-di-tengah-penguatan-sinergi-pengendalian-inflasi).

Those percentages describe a consumer-price index. They are not a monthly rupiah budget for an expat household, nor an instruction to increase every rent or professional-service fee by the same percentage.

---

## Property costs: start with the actual records

For a property-related budget, request the current assessment or bill, payment history and an explanation of any outstanding amount. If you rent, check the contract's allocation of costs rather than assume every property charge belongs in the tenant's budget.

Badung's official public-service portal lists a PBB arrears-checking service linked to E-Palapa. Bapenda Badung also publishes authorised payment channels. Use the channel applicable to the property's jurisdiction and verify the bill before paying. [Badung PBB checking service](https://pelayanan-publik.badungkab.go.id/service/cek-tunggakan-pbb/cc58f030-d0ef-4d15-8946-1afb3e2549ee) and [official payment information](https://bapenda.badungkab.go.id/pembayaran-pajak).

This article does not apply one tax rate, penalty or payment procedure to every property in Bali.

---

## Accounting and tax help: compare the scope

Ask for a written quotation that identifies what is included: a personal filing, company bookkeeping, recurring reporting, payroll support or work on foreign-income records. Confirm the reporting period, deliverables and treatment of additional work.

A PT PMA service package should not be treated as the price of a personal tax return. Have the adviser assess the person's or company's actual obligations before paying for a service. No market-wide accountant fee has been independently established for this guide.

---

## Flights and everyday spending

Price travel for the actual route and dates. Compare the complete booking total, including the baggage and flexibility you need, and add airport transport separately. A historical Bali-Jakarta fare is not a current offer.

For household spending, collect dated quotes for rent, electricity, water, internet, groceries, transport, waste collection, fitness and healthcare. Note which items are included in a rental package and which are billed separately.

## Bali Zero Take

### Our Analysis

The useful budget is a record of evidence: an amount, a billing period, what it includes and when it was checked. Keep household living costs separate from company setup and operating costs.

Compare at least two realistic spending scenarios using actual quotations. Avoid filling gaps with an old online average simply because it looks precise.
