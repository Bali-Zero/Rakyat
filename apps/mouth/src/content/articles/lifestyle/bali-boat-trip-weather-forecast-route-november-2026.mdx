## What to check for your own booking

Our recommendation is to identify the departure port, arrival port, travel date and sailing time before checking information. Ask the operator for the current status of that particular sailing and review the relevant official weather information.

Do not treat a screenshot without its date and area as current guidance. A forecast for waters south of Bali should not automatically be applied to the Bali Strait, Lombok Strait or every route serving nearby islands.

---

## Keep forecast and operating decision separate

A weather forecast describes expected conditions. It is not, by itself, evidence that a harbor has closed or that a booked departure has been cancelled.

Use current information from the operator and relevant authorities for the operational status. If plans change, confirm the options for the specific booking rather than assuming all operators use identical arrangements.

---

## For expat families and hospitality teams

Allow flexibility in travel coordination and keep contact details available. When passing on an advisory, include the issue or validity period, the named waters and the original source.

This article is an example of how to read a dated bulletin, not a clearance to sail. Recheck information close to departure because a saved article cannot establish the conditions or operating decision at the time of your trip.
