## The Facts

Travellers and villa operators should allow flexibility for rain in parts of Bali during 25–27 November 2026. The forecast is location- and time-specific; it does not mean heavy rain everywhere throughout the period.

## The forecast periods that still matter

ANTARA's 24 November report quotes BBMKG Region III Denpasar forecaster Ruth Mahubessy. It identifies these remaining periods:

| Period, WITA | Areas and reported forecast |
| --- | --- |
| 25 November to 26 November, 08:00 | Moderate rain potential in Badung, Bangli, Buleleng, Karangasem and Tabanan. |
| 26 November to 27 November, 08:00 | Heavy-rain alert for Badung, Buleleng and Tabanan; moderate-rain potential in Bangli, Denpasar, Karangasem, Gianyar and Klungkung. |

The report also mentions possible rain and thunder at Bedugul, Kintamani and Besakih on 25 November.

A separate period ending at 08:00 WITA on 25 November has already expired and should not be presented as the current warning. Forecasts can change after publication.

## Before leaving for a trip

Check the latest BMKG forecast for the destination and the hours of travel. Ask your operator about actual service conditions if your itinerary involves boats, exposed trails or other weather-sensitive activities.

Build time flexibility into transfers and avoid treating a general forecast as confirmation of a road closure or a cancellation. Those require current information from the relevant operator or authority.

If thunder or heavy rain develops, follow local instructions and move activities to an appropriate sheltered setting. A forecast for one district does not describe every road or neighbourhood within it.

## A practical villa-operations checklist

Operators can review straightforward preparations:

- Check that drains and gutters are clear where safely accessible.
- Look for slippery paths and poorly lit outdoor steps.
- Move vulnerable outdoor equipment and furnishings under cover.
- Confirm how guests can contact staff during a disruption.
- Prepare an indoor alternative for planned outdoor activities.
- Check upcoming transfers with the service provider.
- Keep a record of actual maintenance issues and guest communications.

These steps are operational preparation, not evidence that a particular property is unsafe or will flood.

## Where to get updates

Use the [BBMKG Region III Denpasar website](https://bbmkg3.bmkg.go.id/), its official @bmkgbali channel or the InfoBMKG application for current updates.
