## The Facts

Bali Post reported on 22 November 2026 that Buleleng’s acting culture and tourism office head, Ketut Suwarmawan, was discussing potential holiday packages with tourism businesses, including PHRI. The report expressly describes the packages as still being explored.

The discussion concerns the Christmas and New Year period and efforts to promote North Bali destinations. It is not evidence that a particular package, price or departure has been confirmed.

---

## In Practice

For a North Bali operator, start with products you can actually deliver. Confirm transport time, accommodation availability, attraction opening arrangements, guide capacity and a weather alternative. Agree which business is responsible for each part before advertising a combined offer.

For expat families planning a trip, ask for a written itinerary with the actual operator, inclusions, cancellation terms and contact details. Distinguish a destination promotion from a confirmed reservation.

Hotels and travel businesses can prepare clear guest information about the area while avoiding unsupported promises about demand. A regional visitor count does not translate directly into occupancy at an individual property, and a planned promotion does not guarantee sales.
