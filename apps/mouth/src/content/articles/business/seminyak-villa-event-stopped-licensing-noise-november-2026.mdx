## The Facts

Bali Post reported on 19 November 2026 on enforcement involving a Seminyak accommodation property following residents’ complaints. Bali Aktual’s 22 November follow-up identified the location as Villa LataLiana and said the integrated Badung team stopped event and nightlife activities on 19 November.

The follow-up attributed the issue to noisy activities and permissions that did not list event organisation and alcohol provision. It also reported that the operator had committed not to hold activities disturbing the neighbourhood. These statements describe the reported case; they are not a finding about every event venue in Bali.

---

## In Practice

For a business planning a launch, reception or paid gathering, begin with an activity sheet: guest numbers, music, hours, alcohol, equipment, vendors and whether the event is commercial. Ask the venue to explain which permissions cover that specific plan rather than accepting a generic assurance that it is a licensed villa.

Separate the accommodation operator from the event organiser and suppliers. Put responsibilities for sound, neighbours, access, waste and cancellation into the written agreement. A booking confirmation should not be your only evidence that the planned activities have been reviewed.

Where the documents do not match the plan, clarify the position with the relevant authority and qualified adviser before making non-refundable commitments. These are practical precautions, not a claim that every private gathering needs the same approvals.
