## The Facts

PropertyGuru's official announcement lists Bali developments among winners at its 12th Indonesia Property Awards, held in Jakarta on 13 November 2026.

The organizer names Paradisus by Meliá Bali as Best Luxury Hotel / Resort Development (Bali), Hideaway Pandawa as Best Investment Villa Development, and Akkulla Villa Bali as Best Villa Development. These are the organizer's award categories, not Bali Zero investment ratings.

The announcement describes a judging process overseen by HLB Indonesia. It does not replace the documents needed to assess an individual purchase or the purchaser's proposed use.

---

## In Practice

When an award appears in a sales presentation, check the exact project, category and year. An award to a developer or a different phase should not be assumed to cover the unit being offered.

Ask what the award assessed. Design, hospitality experience, development quality and a buyer's contractual protections are different questions. Even an investment-labelled award is not a guarantee of a particular rental return.

For the transaction itself, request documents identifying the seller, unit, underlying land interest, construction or handover status, and the arrangements for management and maintenance.

## Bali Zero Take

### Our Analysis

Awards can be a useful starting point for discovering projects. They should be one input in a decision, alongside independent document review and a site inspection.
