## TL;DR

A HIPMI forum speaker urged better product quality, permits and investor reporting. The remarks are industry observations, with no finding against a named project.

---

## The Facts

Bali's local property developers should study competitors' strengths while improving their own products and management, Ciputra Beach Resort Project Director Angela Hanggi told a HIPMI property forum, according to NusaBali.

The report describes the HIPMI Real Estate Development Program at UC Silver, Batubulan, on Saturday, 7 November 2026. Hanggi highlighted product concepts, design, technology, international marketing and brand development as areas where local firms could learn from foreign competitors.

She also raised concerns about nominee arrangements and construction beginning before permits are complete, saying such practices could involve local developers as well. These are attributed industry observations, not findings against a named project.

The discussion included regular occupancy and revenue reporting to investors, professional property management, customer service and attention to infrastructure when selecting a development location. It was an industry forum, rather than an announcement of a new property law.

---

## In Practice

Nationality is not a substitute for examining a developer's documents and performance. Neither foreign branding nor a local reputation establishes that a project is properly licensed, financially sound or likely to achieve its advertised returns.

BKPM's January 2026 announcement on the Bali Investment Desk independently identifies building approvals, functional-worthiness certificates, environmental approvals and investment compliance among its supervision concerns. The documents applicable to a particular project depend on the proposed use and its stage.

## Bali Zero Take

### Our Analysis

The most useful benchmark for buyers is the quality of evidence a developer can provide. Ask what the project is permitted to do, who is contractually responsible for construction and management, and how actual operating performance will be reported.

A marketing forecast should be distinguishable from historical results. If occupancy or revenue figures are shown, the buyer should know the property, period, calculation basis and supporting records. That is a due-diligence recommendation, rather than a claim that any nationality of developer performs better.
