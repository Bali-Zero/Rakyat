## The Facts

## An existing integration story, not a new November deadline
Reports about linking business licensing with tax compliance should be read with their original dates. [Ortax covered BKPM's OSS integration announcement on 11 March 2026](https://ortax.org/bkpm-integrasikan-sistem-perizinan-dan-kepatuhan-pajak-melalui-online-single-submission), describing connections between licensing, taxpayer information and company identity.
That reporting provides context for a practical records review. It does not establish a new universal filing deadline in November, an automatic cancellation of every mismatched licence or an entitlement to tax incentives.
## What the tax authority actually checks
[DJP's current KSWP service guide](https://pajak.go.id/panduan-layanan-pajak/konten/layanan-digital/2025/layanan-administrasi/pemenuhan-kewajiban-perpajakan/konfirmasi-status-wajib-pajak-%28kswp%29) explains that government agencies use taxpayer-status confirmation before providing certain public services.
For a valid status, the guide identifies matching taxpayer name and NPWP information, together with annual income-tax returns for the two latest tax years that have already become the taxpayer's filing obligations. The qualification about an existing obligation matters: do not turn it into a claim that every newly formed company must already have two years of returns.
The guide also provides an official application workflow through the taxpayer portal. Use the current instructions for the company and its authorised representative rather than an old screenshot from another service.
## Prepare evidence before an application
A useful internal check is to place the company's latest identity documents, tax-registration record and relevant filing receipts together. Identify which person can explain a discrepancy and whether a correction has merely been submitted or has actually been accepted.
Keep a record of the application reference, the service being requested and any exact validation message. This makes a follow-up more useful than a general statement that OSS is not working.
## Separate a system problem from a compliance finding
A page that fails to load or a temporary validation error is not, by itself, proof of tax non-compliance. Equally, a successful login does not establish that every licensing requirement has been satisfied.
For a Bali PT PMA, the practical question is which requirements apply to its particular activity and application. Check those requirements through the official channel, reconcile any identified discrepancy and retain the evidence. This checklist is editorial guidance for preparation; it does not replace a decision from the responsible authority.
