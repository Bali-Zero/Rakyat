## The Facts

Strong foreign-investment headlines can attract attention to Bali. They do not tell a prospective buyer whether a particular villa, office or business will earn the return being advertised.

A NEXT Indonesia Center analysis published on 6 November 2026 describes foreign capital supporting national investment while domestic investment weakened. Its statistical discussion covers January–September 2026, rather than a single month.

## What the report says

NEXT reports national foreign investment, or PMA, of Rp777.8 trillion over the nine-month period, up 20.67% year on year. It reports domestic investment contractions of 7.77% in the second quarter and 7.48% in the third quarter.

For Bali, it identifies Rp8.5 trillion within the combined housing, industrial-estates and offices category. The figure should retain that category label: it is not a measure of villa rental income, foreign buyers' profits or total investment across every Bali sector.

These figures are attributed to NEXT's analysis of investment data. This article has not independently reproduced the underlying BKPM statistical tables. The report's interpretation should be read as analysis, not an official promise about future performance.

## Why PT PMA investors should separate three questions

First, what does the statistic measure? Investment realization describes capital committed or implemented within a reporting framework. A project's operating profit is a different measure.

Second, what business will the company actually conduct? Buying an interest in a development, operating accommodation and providing management services are different commercial activities. A headline about foreign capital does not establish the licensing route for a particular company.

Third, what supports the projected return? Ask for assumptions about occupancy, pricing, maintenance, staffing, taxes, management fees and financing. A forecast should identify both its evidence and its sensitivity to weaker demand.

## In practice: a document checklist

Before transferring money, assemble the proposed transaction documents, ownership or lease information, the company's business scope and the proposed operating model. Identify which party is responsible for permits, construction, management and ongoing expenses.

For a revenue forecast, request a breakdown of gross receipts and each cost deduction. Check whether a quoted return is before or after fees, whether it depends on a future resale, and who is expected to meet any contractual promise. Have the documents independently reviewed before relying on the marketing presentation.

## Bali Zero take

Use regional investment data as context for asking better questions. Do not substitute it for a project-level review. Two developments in the same province can have different legal structures, operating costs and commercial prospects.

The useful conclusion for a Bali PT PMA is to match the proposed investment, actual business activity and supporting documents before committing funds. Neither foreign-capital growth nor a popular location guarantees licensing approval or a positive return.
