---

## In Practice

Identify the legal entity signing the purchase contract and the entity responsible for building work. Ask who supervises quality and who can certify that a payment milestone has been achieved.

Request a specification schedule covering materials, fixtures, dimensions and acceptable substitutions. A clear procedure for approving changes helps prevent a broad promise such as “premium finish” from becoming the only point of reference.

Ask for the inspection and handover process, the defect-reporting window and the remedy if faults remain unresolved. Check whether independent inspection is permitted before the final payment.

## Bali Zero Take

### Our Analysis

Keeping work within a group can simplify coordination, but it does not remove the need for documented accountability. Outsourcing construction does not automatically make a project better or worse either.

Compare the actual controls: written specifications, progress evidence, independent review, payment protections and remedies. Do not assume a business model alone proves construction quality or timely delivery.
