## The Facts

Bali Post reported on 22 November 2026 that Karangasem Regent I Gusti Putu Parwata invited tourism investment to spread across the regency’s eight districts. He spoke at a PHRI gathering in Candidasa on 20 November and discussed obstacles involving permits, water, roads, lighting and workforce needs.

The report describes a development invitation and a commitment to coordination. It is not a published exemption from permitting requirements or confirmation that every proposed foreign investment activity is eligible.

---

## In Practice

A useful first assessment begins with the exact parcel and business plan. Ask about access, dependable water supply, wastewater arrangements, electricity, staffing and how guests would reach the site. Put these questions into the operating budget before relying on a promotional description of an emerging destination.

For a PT PMA proposal, ask a qualified adviser to check the activity classification, investment route and location requirements. Keep discussions with the local government separate from the documents that actually authorise the project.

Consider the surrounding community as part of feasibility: delivery traffic, staff transport, shared access and water demand can affect both operations and relationships. A realistic costed plan is more informative than a claim that the area is the next guaranteed growth market.

---

## What This Does Not Establish

The announcement does not guarantee approval, completed infrastructure, demand or investment returns. Any official commitment relevant to a particular project should be documented and checked before funds are committed.
