## The Facts

A request for court enforcement has brought a Bali property dispute back into the news. JPNN Bali reported on 14 November that lawyers representing Magnum Estate had urged prosecutors to act in a case involving Budiman Tiang. The report covered statements made by lawyer Agus Samijaya on 13 November.

According to JPNN, the legal team said it had written to prosecution authorities and the President. This establishes the team's publicly reported request; it does not independently establish the current enforcement status.

## The other side's reported position

In an earlier report dated 6 October, Suara.com described Budiman Tiang's lawyers preparing a judicial-review application. Lawyer Tommy Tri Yunanto alleged errors in the earlier judicial reasoning and said the team was preparing its arguments and new evidence. Those are the defence team's claims, not findings adopted by this article. The report does not establish that a later application was filed or decided.

The original judgment and an up-to-date prosecution record have not been independently reviewed for this article. No conclusion is therefore offered about the merits, current enforcement position, or ownership of any property.

## A practical document check for investors

When a dispute affects a proposed investment, request the case number, complete judgment and latest procedural records rather than relying solely on statements from either side. Keep agreements, payment evidence, company documents and formal correspondence indexed separately.

Ask your adviser to explain which documents relate to the transaction you are considering. A report about criminal proceedings should not be treated as proof resolving every contractual or ownership question concerning a development.
