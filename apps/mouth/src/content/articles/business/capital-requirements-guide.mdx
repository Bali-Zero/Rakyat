# PT PMA Capital Requirements Indonesia 2026: Investment Guide

Understanding capital requirements is crucial for foreign investors in Indonesia. Here's what you need to know.

## Capital Structure Overview

<InfoCard variant="note" title="Two Types of Capital">
**PT PMA has two capital figures:**

1. **Authorized Capital** (Modal Dasar) - Maximum capital company can have
2. **Paid-up Capital** (Modal Disetor) - Actually invested capital

Both have minimum requirements for foreign investment.

</InfoCard>

---

## Current Requirements

### Standard PT PMA Minimums

| Capital Type         | Minimum Amount                                            |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Total Investment** | More than IDR 10 billion (~USD 625,000) per KBLI/location |
| **Paid-up Capital**  | IDR 2.5 billion (reduced from IDR 10 billion in Oct 2025) |
| **Per Shareholder**  | IDR 10 million minimum                                    |

<InfoCard variant="warning" title="2025 Changes (BKPM Regulation 5/2025)">
**Important change:**

Since October 2025, BKPM Regulation 5/2025 (Article 26) reduced the minimum paid-up capital to IDR 2.5 billion, which must stay in the company account for at least 12 months. The total investment commitment of more than IDR 10 billion per 5-digit KBLI code per project location (excluding land and buildings, with sector exceptions) is unchanged.

Before October 2025, the paid-up capital had to equal the full IDR 10 billion — older guides still quote that figure.

</InfoCard>

## Investment vs Paid-Up Capital

### What's the Difference?

| Concept            | Meaning                                                      |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Total Investment   | All resources for the business (capital + loans + equipment) |
| Paid-Up Capital    | Actual cash/assets contributed by shareholders               |
| Authorized Capital | Maximum shares the company can issue                         |

## Capital Calculation

### What Counts Toward Investment?

| Counts                       | Doesn't Count                              |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Cash deposits                | Land/buildings (excl. from IDR 10B min.*)  |
| Equipment & machinery        | Future earnings                            |
| Intellectual property        | Goodwill                                   |
| Initial inventory / opex     | Projected revenue                          |
| Feasibility & setup capex    | Unvalued intangibles                       |

*\*Under BKPM Reg. 5/2025 (and prior BKPM Reg. 4/2021), the >IDR 10 billion investment minimum per 5-digit KBLI per project location excludes land and buildings, except for specific property/construction/industrial KBLIs.*

## Asset Contribution

### Non-Cash Capital

<InfoCard variant="tip" title="Contributing Assets">
**You can contribute non-cash assets — subject to BKPM land/building exclusions:**

- Equipment (machinery, vehicles) — counts toward investment
- Intellectual property (patents, trademarks) — counts with appraisal
- Real property (land, buildings) — can be corporate equity, **but is EXCLUDED from the > IDR 10B BKPM minimum investment test for standard sectors**

**Requirements:**

- Must be appraised by certified appraiser
- Appraisal report submitted to notary
- Assets must be transferred to company
- Documented in company deed

</InfoCard>

### Appraisal Process

| Step | Action                      |
| ---- | --------------------------- |
| 1    | Engage certified appraiser  |
| 2    | Provide asset documentation |
| 3    | Appraiser inspects/values   |
| 4    | Receive appraisal report    |
| 5    | Submit to notary            |
| 6    | Record in company deed      |
