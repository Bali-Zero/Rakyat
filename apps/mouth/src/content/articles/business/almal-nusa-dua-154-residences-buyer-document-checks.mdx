## In Practice

For a prospective buyer, the next useful step is a document request. Ask which legal entity will sign the agreement, what precisely is being acquired, and which land or unit documents support that interest.

Request the PBG itself and have a suitably qualified adviser compare its holder, location, scope and conditions with the unit and works being marketed. A reference to an approval in a press release does not replace examining the underlying document.

Ask separately about construction milestones, completion conditions and the approvals required for the intended operation. Do not treat a groundbreaking ceremony as evidence that a finished unit is ready for occupation or commercial use.

For a professionally managed residence, request the actual management terms. Questions include owner-use arrangements, management charges, reserve contributions, calculation and distribution of income, reporting rights, termination and resale. These are review questions, not claims about this project's contractual terms.
