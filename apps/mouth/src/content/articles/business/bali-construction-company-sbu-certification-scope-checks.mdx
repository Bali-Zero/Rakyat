## The Facts

An official Public Works announcement describes the launch of OSS-connected construction business certification on 5 October 2021. Although that announcement has resurfaced in the news queue, it is historical material, not a new November 2026 licensing reform.

The current review must also account for later rules. Government Regulation 28/2025 replaced Government Regulation 5/2021 on risk-based business licensing. The official BPK legal database lists Public Works Minister Regulation 6/2025 as in force from 30 December 2025, covering business standards, supervision and sanctions in the public-works sector.

Those developments mean an old launch announcement is not a complete application manual. Applicants should check the current official procedure for their business activity, including any applicable transition arrangements, rather than assuming every step still follows the 2021 workflow.

---

## In Practice

For a Bali property owner selecting a construction company, begin with the exact legal entity that will sign the contract. Compare its name and registration details across the proposed contract, business records and certification documents.

Public Works guidance distinguishes construction consulting, construction work and integrated construction work. The scope of the proposed service therefore matters. Request the contractor's relevant SBU, or business-entity certificate, and check its classification, qualification, validity and current status against the official records.

A certificate held by a related company should not simply be treated as the signing company's certificate. Ask who is responsible for the work and how any subcontracted services will be documented.

Keep the contractor's business qualifications separate from the property's own approvals. Checking the contractor does not, by itself, establish that the planned building or its intended use has all necessary approvals.

## Bali Zero Take

### Our Analysis

The practical question is whether the proposed service, contracting entity and supporting records match. A business identification number alone should not substitute for examining the applicable sector requirements.

Likewise, a person or company commissioning a villa project should not automatically be described as a construction-service provider merely because it owns the project. The role being performed determines which requirements need to be assessed.
