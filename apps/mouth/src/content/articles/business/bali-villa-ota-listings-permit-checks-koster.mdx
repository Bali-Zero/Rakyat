## The Facts

## What Koster said

Bali Governor Wayan Koster urged online accommodation-marketing platforms not to advertise villas, rental houses and other accommodation without business permits. He spoke at the ASITA Bali regional conference in Jimbaran on 16 October 2026.

ANTARA reported the request on the day of the event. The Bali provincial government's report, published on 23 October, also highlighted his concern about unlicensed accommodation marketed through online travel agencies and its impact on fair competition and tourism quality.

## What the statement does not establish

The reports describe a public request to platforms and accommodation businesses. They do not establish a newly enacted platform regulation, a confirmed deadline for removing listings, or evidence that mass delisting has already taken place.

Owners should distinguish an official policy statement from a platform's actual published requirements. Do not assume that every booking site has introduced an identical document-upload process or that a listing has become unlawful merely because this statement was reported.

## Turn the headline into a document review

For a villa investor or operator, the practical response is to assemble a consistent property file. The following questions are an editorial due-diligence checklist, not a list of new requirements created by the governor's remarks:

- Which legal entity actually operates the accommodation?
- Do its business records identify the activity and location being marketed?
- Who keeps the relevant business-licensing documents and tracks their status?
- What do the building-use records say, and does the current operation match them?
- Who is responsible for checking the applicable local tax registration, reporting and payment records?
- Does the management agreement clearly allocate document maintenance and responses to platform requests?

Have the relevant adviser or local authority confirm which approvals and obligations apply to the property's actual category and operation. A document checklist copied from another villa may not resolve the facts of this one.

## Owner, manager and listing account should tell the same story

A management company may handle bookings while another entity operates the accommodation or owns the asset. That arrangement needs a clear paper trail.

As a practical example, compare the operating entity in the management agreement with the entity named in the licensing records and the information supplied to the booking platform. If those names differ, establish the documented reason and responsibility rather than assuming that one company's papers cover the entire arrangement.

Keep a dated record of requests received directly from each platform. A screenshot of an active listing proves that the property is being advertised; it is not an official confirmation that all business requirements are satisfied.

## For a prospective buyer or investor

Ask to see the relevant records before relying on projected rental income. Record any missing information and make the commercial implications clear in the review.

A forecast based on OTA bookings should identify who manages the account and who is responsible for keeping its information accurate. This is useful investment diligence regardless of whether a platform changes its policies following the governor's request.

## Bali Zero take

Koster's comments make accommodation compliance a current business issue. The evidence supports checking the file now. It does not support claiming that a new island-wide ban or automatic removal programme is already in force.
