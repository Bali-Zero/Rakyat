## The Facts

In a release dated 19 November 2026, the Ministry of Investment and Downstreaming/BKPM described a discussion held in Jakarta on 18 November about inclusive investment and disability-owned MSMEs. It highlighted business matching, capacity, standards, business legality and financing as areas for connecting small suppliers with larger buyers.

The announcement promotes meaningful commercial participation. It does not itself establish a new universal percentage of procurement that every PT PMA must allocate to disability-owned suppliers.

---

## In Practice

Hotels, restaurants and other Bali businesses can start with an actual purchasing need rather than a publicity pledge. Write down the product or service specification, expected volume, delivery schedule and payment terms. Ask prospective suppliers what capacity and accessibility arrangements would make the relationship workable.

Use the same clear quality and commercial standards for all suppliers, while avoiding unnecessary barriers in the tender or communication process. A modest trial order with a realistic timeline can be more useful than a promise the buyer cannot sustain.

Document agreed responsibilities, feedback and timely payment. Procurement staff should distinguish this practical sourcing exercise from any separate partnership obligations that may apply to their own company or licence.

## What This Does Not Establish

The release does not establish that a new OSS supplier directory is already live, that inclusive sourcing automatically satisfies every ESG requirement, or that participation guarantees a government-backed contract. Those claims need separate evidence.
