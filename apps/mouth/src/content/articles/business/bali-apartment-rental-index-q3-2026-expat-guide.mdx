## The Facts

Bali’s apartment rental market has attracted attention after a reported 23.18% annual increase in its rental index. The figure is useful market context for expats negotiating accommodation and investors reviewing assumptions. It does not show that every apartment’s rent rose by that amount.

## What the report says

ANTARA’s 20 November 2026 report, quoting Bank Indonesia Bali head Achris Sarwani, gives a 23.18% year-on-year rise in the apartment rental index for the third quarter of 2026.

Commercial-property statistics may cover different segments and comparison periods. Before comparing headlines, check the measure, geographic coverage and whether the change is annual or quarterly. Reports differ in how they describe the comparison period for the broader commercial-property contraction, so that separate figure is not used here.

This article relies on ANTARA’s attributed account of Bank Indonesia’s figures. The index is not a property-by-property rent survey conducted by Bali Zero.

## If you are renting

Use the headline as a reason to compare current offers, not as an automatic justification for a proposed rent increase. Match location, size, condition, lease length and included services.

Ask for the complete cost of occupancy: rent, deposit, utilities, internet, cleaning, parking and any management charges. A cheaper advertised monthly price may exclude items included elsewhere.

Compare the payment schedule too. Twelve months paid upfront and a flexible monthly agreement have different cash-flow and cancellation consequences. Get repair responsibilities, renewal terms, deposit deductions and termination arrangements in writing.

Before agreeing to a renewal, ask the landlord to identify the actual proposed price and contract changes. An index percentage is not itself a term in your agreement.

## If you are investing

Do not insert 23.18% into a villa’s revenue forecast as an expected annual uplift. An apartment rental index does not establish a particular villa’s occupancy, nightly rate or net income.

Build a property-specific model using comparable units and distinguish asking prices from achieved rents. Record whether the evidence concerns long-term residential rentals, serviced apartments or short stays.

Stress-test vacancy, maintenance and management costs. A higher headline rent can coexist with lower net cash flow if operating expenses rise or the property sits empty longer.

For a PT PMA, keep the property decision connected to the company’s actual planned activity and the documents required for that activity. Market demand and permission to operate are separate questions.

## A useful comparison sheet

For each candidate property, record the source date, location, unit type, contract length, included services, payment schedule and total annual cost. Add a column for information that is still missing.

That produces a more useful negotiation tool than treating one aggregate percentage as the correct price for every neighbourhood. The Q3 figure describes a reported market movement; your decision still depends on the unit and agreement in front of you.
