# How to Register a PT PMA in Indonesia: Step by Step 2026

Registering a PT PMA (Perseroan Terbatas Penanaman Modal Asing) is the standard way for foreigners to establish a legal business entity in Indonesia. Here's exactly how to do it.

<InfoCard
  variant="danger"
  title="2026 Compliance Update: Mandatory Annual Reporting"
>

**New Regulation 49/2025:** As of 2026, all PT PMAs are now required to submit
notarized Annual Reports (GMS Approval) via the SABH system. Failure to comply
results in a written warning, then a block on your SABH/AHU access (not NIB suspension). Additionally, stricter UBO (Ultimate
Beneficial Owner) verification is now mandatory for all corporate changes.
[Read the full PT PMA Compliance Mandate
→](/business/indonesia-corporate-reporting-mandate-regulation-49-2025)

</InfoCard>

## Tips for Success

<InfoCard variant="tip" title="Registration Tips">
  1. **KBLI first**: Choose eligible open codes before drafting your Deed
  2. **Plan capital**: Deposit IDR 2.5B paid-up per company and structure >IDR 10B investment (excluding land & buildings)
  3. **Use professionals**: Proper KBLI/zoning checks prevent costly Akta revisions
  4. **Timeline**: Allow 6–8 weeks end-to-end
  5. **Director presence**: May be required for local corporate bank account opening

## Regulatory Context & Policy Notes

<InfoCard
  variant="note"
  title="Policy Update: Paid-Up Capital & Bali Zoning Rules (2025–2026)"
>

Based on **BKPM Regulation 5/2025 (Article 26)** and active **OSS-RBA / DPMPTSP Bali** guidance:

</InfoCard>

### Key Corporate Capital & Structuring Realities

1. **Paid-up capital set at IDR 2.5B per company** — Since October 2025 (BKPM Reg 5/2025 Art. 26), paid-up capital at incorporation was adjusted to **IDR 2.5 billion per company** (subject to a 12-month lock-up unless spent on verified business operations), while total investment commitment remains **> IDR 10 billion per 5-digit KBLI per location excluding land and buildings**.
2. **Closed & restricted KBLIs in Bali** — Several accommodation/service codes (such as `55203` Villas, `55201` Pondok Wisata, `55106` Non-Star Hotels, and `79903` Tour Guides) are `0% PMA` nationwide under Perpres 49/2021, and Low/Medium-Low codes face Bali's 2026 PMA moratorium (`Surat Gubernur B.27.000/642/PM/DPMPTSP`).
3. **Commercial premise vs. Virtual Office** — DPMPTSP Bali actively rejects Virtual Office setups for restricted/moratorium-affected KBLI filings; verify physical zoning (PKKPR) before leasing.
4. **Investor KITAS (E28A) threshold** — To qualify for an E28A Investor KITAS without RPTKA, each foreign applicant must hold at least **IDR 10 billion** in personal shares; directors/shareholders below IDR 10B require an **E23 Working KITAS**.

**Always verify current OSS requirements on oss.go.id or bkpm.go.id before committing funds.**
