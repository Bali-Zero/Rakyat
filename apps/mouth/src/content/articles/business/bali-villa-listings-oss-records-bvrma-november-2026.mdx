## The Facts

Warta Bali reported on 19 November 2026 that Bali Villa Rental and Management Association chairman I Kadek Adnyana discussed approximately 37,000 digitally listed villas and around 17,000 properties recorded in OSS at an industry meeting at the Bali Provincial Tourism Office. The figures are attributed to the association, not presented here as an independently audited count.

The comparison raises a useful question about the relationship between marketing and business records. It does not provide a property-by-property reconciliation. Multiple listings, differences in coverage and the meaning of each dataset must be resolved before estimating the number of unlicensed properties.

---

## In Practice

For a foreign owner or PT PMA manager, start with one property rather than an island-wide percentage. Put the listing address, operator name, booking recipient and management agreement side by side. Ask the operator to identify the corresponding NIB, registered activities and documents supporting the actual accommodation operation.

Keep commercial and property questions separate: who takes bookings, who operates the business, and which documents relate to the land and building? Ask an appropriate adviser to review any mismatch instead of assuming that a platform listing, a company registration or a management contract answers every question.

A practical review folder can contain the current listing, operator details, licensing records, building documents and a list of unresolved points with the person responsible for checking each one. This is a suggested review method, not a new government filing requirement.

---

## What This Does Not Establish

The arithmetic difference is not proof that 20,000 unique villas are illegal. The cited report does not establish a new nationwide deadline, an amnesty or an automatic penalty for every listed owner.
