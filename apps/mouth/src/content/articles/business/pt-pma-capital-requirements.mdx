# PT PMA Capital Requirements: What You Need to Invest 2026

The **IDR 10 billion** (~$625,000 USD) minimum investment for PT PMA is one of the most misunderstood requirements. Let's break down what it actually means.

## The Two Key Numbers

| Requirement          | Amount                                                        | What It Means                                                |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Total Investment** | > IDR 10 billion per 5-digit KBLI/location (excl. land & bldgs) | Minimum total project investment plan (Nilai Investasi)      |
| **Paid-up Capital**  | IDR 2.5 billion min **per company** (BKPM Reg 5/2025 Art. 26) | Minimum issued/paid-up capital deposited (12-month lock-up)  |

<InfoCard variant="important" title="BKPM Reg 5/2025 Clarification">

Under **Perka BKPM 5/2025 Art. 26**, the minimum paid-up capital (*Modal Disetor*) is **IDR 2.5 billion per PT PMA company** (regardless of number of shareholders), subject to a 12-month lock-up in the corporate account unless used for verified business expenses. Meanwhile, the **> IDR 10 billion** total investment commitment (*Nilai Investasi*) remains required per 5-digit KBLI per project location and explicitly **excludes land and buildings** (for standard non-developer sectors).

</InfoCard>

## Common Questions About Capital

<InfoCard variant="note" title="FAQ">
**Q: Do I need IDR 10B in cash in my bank account on day 1?**
A: No. Under BKPM Regulation 5/2025 Art. 26, minimum paid-up capital is **IDR 2.5 billion per company** (subject to the 12-month lock-up unless spent on documented corporate operations). The remaining investment commitment up to > IDR 10 billion (excluding land/buildings) can be deployed via equipment, working capital, and operational expenditure over your investment timeline.

**Q: Can I use it after depositing?**
A: Paid-up capital is subject to a 12-month lock-up period unless disbursed for legitimate, documented business assets or operations of the PT PMA.

**Q: What if I can't meet > IDR 10B investment?**
A: Options include:

- Joint venture / local partnership (PT PMDN requires domestic equity rules)
- Representative Office (KPPA — non-revenue)
- Defer incorporation until > IDR 10B investment plan is viable

**Q: Is there any way around the minimum?**
A: No legitimate workaround. Regulations require > IDR 10B total investment per 5-digit KBLI/location (excluding land/buildings) and IDR 2.5B minimum paid-up capital per PT PMA company (higher in special sectors like finance or mining).

</InfoCard>
