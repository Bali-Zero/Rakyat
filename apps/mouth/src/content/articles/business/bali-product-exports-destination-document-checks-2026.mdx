## The Facts

JPNN Bali reported on 19 November, citing Bali Quarantine head Heri Yuwono, that 2,708 plant-export certificates had been issued through October 2026. The report described agricultural-product exports reaching 85 destination countries.

These are reported certification and destination figures for the period described, not a count of all Bali exporters or proof that every product can enter all 85 markets. The report also discusses differences in destination-country sanitary and treatment requirements.

---

## Start with the product and destination

Our recommendation is to prepare a shipment brief identifying the product, quantity, origin, processing or treatment and destination. Ask the relevant quarantine authority and the destination-side importer which documents apply to that specific shipment.

A generic request for an export certificate is less useful than a precise description. If the product or destination changes, revisit the document list rather than copying the previous shipment's file.

---

## Questions for the commercial arrangement

Agree who will obtain each document and when it must be ready. Clarify how the exporter and importer will exchange any inspection or treatment information and how discrepancies will be handled before dispatch.

Keep the final document set together with the commercial and transport records. This is a suggested coordination practice, not a universal checklist for every regulated commodity.

---

## For an expat-owned Bali business

Ask the team to distinguish company-level preparations from consignment-specific requirements. A positive export trend is useful context, but it does not determine the conditions for your own goods.

The news report alone cannot confirm that a particular shipment is ready to leave Indonesia or enter another country. Obtain the applicable requirements before committing to a delivery promise.
