## In Practice

Ask the seller or agent to identify the route on a map and explain which sections are publicly accessible. Test the journey from the actual property entrance to the places you regularly use, rather than from a nearby landmark.

Repeat the journey at the times that matter to you. Record the date, time and conditions. One quiet afternoon trip cannot establish the normal commute during school runs, rain or evening traffic.

Look at the final approach as well as the main road. Check the width of the lane, vehicle access, parking, drainage and the arrangements for deliveries or emergency access. Where access depends on another parcel, ask an independent adviser to review the relevant documents.
