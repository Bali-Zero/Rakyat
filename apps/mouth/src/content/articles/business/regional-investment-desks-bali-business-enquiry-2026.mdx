## The Facts

BKPM announced a regional investment desk in Balikpapan in a release dated 17 November 2026. Its geographic focus is Kalimantan, covering the island’s five provinces. The release places the initiative in a wider sequence of regional facilitation activities, including earlier activity in Bali.

The Balikpapan announcement should therefore not be presented as the opening of a new office in Bali. It also does not establish a new blanket approval deadline for Bali companies.

---

## In Practice

For an operator facing a licensing problem, a useful enquiry explains the exact activity, location, application reference and unresolved step. Include the wording of any official request for additional information and a concise timeline. Avoid sending a vague message that the entire permit is “stuck” when one specific requirement needs clarification.

Ask the relevant official channel which authority owns the unresolved issue and what document or action is required next. Keep a copy of the response and distinguish guidance from an actual approval or issued document.

A PT PMA director can assign one person to maintain this case file so repeated enquiries do not contain conflicting versions. This is a practical administrative suggestion, not an additional statutory reporting requirement.

---

## What This Does Not Establish

Investment facilitation does not replace the authority responsible for the underlying decision. The release does not promise that an investment desk can waive substantive requirements, overturn a refusal or approve every application within a fixed period.
