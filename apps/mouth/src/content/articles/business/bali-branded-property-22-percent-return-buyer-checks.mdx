## The Facts

A headline return can make a branded property sound like a straightforward investment. For expats considering a Nusa Dua purchase, the useful question is how that number would become money actually paid to the buyer.

Market Business News reported on 20 November 2026 that promotional material for The One by ALMAL presents returns of up to 22%. That is a reported marketing claim, not a return independently verified by Bali Zero or a guarantee established from an executed buyer contract.

## What is being offered?

The developer’s project page describes a 154-unit development in Nusa Dua and estimates handover in 2028. These are developer statements about a project under development, rather than evidence of completed operations or rental income.

The reporting also distinguishes the hotel brand from the developer and seller. Buyers should obtain the actual brand, management and purchase documents instead of assuming that a familiar hotel name guarantees construction, repayment or investment performance.

## Ask for the calculation behind the percentage

Request a dated cash-flow model identifying the purchase price, occupancy, nightly rate, opening date and all deductions. Ask whether “return” means gross revenue, net operating income, cash distributed to the owner, or a figure that includes projected resale value.

An illustrative example shows why the definition matters: annual gross receipts of USD22,000 on a USD100,000 price equal 22% before expenses. If expenses total USD8,000, the remaining USD14,000 equals 14% before any other applicable costs. These are hypothetical figures, not forecasts for this project.

Include furnishing, maintenance reserves, management fees, insurance, taxes, financing and periods when the unit cannot earn income. Ask how personal-use nights affect the calculation.

## Identify who owes what

Build a simple document list with the legal name of the seller, developer, manager and any party promising payments. For each obligation, record the responsible company, payment trigger, deadline and remedy.

If a salesperson uses the word “guaranteed,” ask for the clause, duration, exclusions and identity of the guarantor. A statement in a presentation should be reconciled with the agreement that the buyer will sign.

Ask an independent adviser to review the land and contractual rights, approvals, construction milestones, payment protection and dispute provisions. A branded development still requires project-specific checks.

## Compare a downside case

Test a later opening, lower occupancy and higher operating costs. Then ask whether the purchase still fits your budget and liquidity needs. Request the assumptions in writing so that later changes can be traced.

Before paying, keep the dated marketing material alongside the final contracts and a written list of unresolved questions. This is a document-review framework, not a conclusion that the project is unsuitable or that its advertised return will occur.
