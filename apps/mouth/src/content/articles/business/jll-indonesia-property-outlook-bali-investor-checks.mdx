## Three themes in the reported analysis

Pasardana's three original labels are “diversifikasi manufaktur”, “pusat data dan infrastruktur digital”, and “optimalisasi dan alih fungsi properti”: manufacturing diversification, data centres and digital infrastructure, and property optimisation or conversion.

The manufacturing paragraph names “Batang, Kendal, dan Semarang”. The special-economic-zone paragraph includes “Jabodetabek, Bali, Batam”. We checked these passages on the linked Pasardana page, whose displayed date is 19 October 2026.

The third theme concerns adapting assets such as older offices and retail buildings. It should not be replaced with an unspecified sustainability theme or presented as a recommendation for every Bali villa.

These are a consultant's market assessments. They are not evidence of a guaranteed price increase, investment return or entitlement to operate a particular business.

## Translate the headline into questions

For a Bali investor, our practical recommendation is to begin with the proposed asset's customer and revenue model. Who would pay to use it, what competing supply exists, and which evidence supports the demand assumptions?

Keep a separate evidence column for signed agreements, observed operating results, comparable listings and forecasts. A forecast should remain identifiable as a forecast when it enters a financial model.

If the proposal involves a different sector or region, prepare a fresh operating plan. Experience with a villa does not, by itself, establish the demand, infrastructure or operating requirements of a warehouse or data-centre project.

## Check the asset before adopting the narrative

Ask the project team to identify the activity, location and documents that support the intended use. Have the relevant advisers assess the ownership structure, licensing and transaction requirements for that specific proposal.

A broad reference to a special economic zone is not proof that a particular parcel is inside it or qualifies for a particular facility. Request the location and eligibility evidence rather than relying on proximity or a description in a sales presentation.

For an existing building, compare the current use with the proposed conversion. Record what further technical, commercial and regulatory assessments are needed before pricing the conversion as a completed opportunity.
