## The Facts

Anantara Residences Ubud Bali is being marketed as a collection of 15 residences in a rainforest and river-valley setting. Minor's official project page describes the residences and lists an opening target in the fourth quarter of 2026.

November media coverage identifies Minor International and WIKA Realty as the developers and places the project in Payangan, north of central Ubud. The coverage describes a launch in October 2026.

A published opening target is not proof that a specific unit has been completed or handed over. This is coverage of a commercial development announcement, not an independent certification of the project or an investment recommendation.

---

## In Practice

A prospective buyer should request a document pack for the exact unit and transaction structure. Start with the contracting entity, the interest being acquired, its duration, and the documents identifying the land and unit.

For a branded residence, separate the purchase agreement from the management arrangement. Ask which services are included, which fees are mandatory, who appoints the operator, and what happens if the brand or operator changes.

If a rental programme is offered, request its current terms. Check owner-use restrictions, booking priority, maintenance reserves, insurance, revenue allocation and the basis for any illustration of returns.
