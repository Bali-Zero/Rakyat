## The Facts

Detik Bali reported on 20 November 2026 that Governor’s Decision 995/03-M/HK/2026 sets the 2027 provincial minimum wage at Rp4,559,287 per month and the provincial sectoral minimum for star-rated hotels at Rp4,644,907, with effect from 1 January 2027. Bali Post subsequently reported the same figures.

This is a material update to earlier coverage of the wage discussions. However, the signed decision has not been independently inspected for this article. The figures are therefore attributed to the published reports; the full legal scope should be confirmed from the official document.

Bali Post reported on 22 November that Badung planned a further discussion of district and sectoral district wage recommendations on 23 November. A provincial announcement should not be treated as proof that every district-level figure has already been finalised.

---

## In Practice

Ask HR to obtain the official decision and identify the applicable provincial, district or sectoral category for each workplace. Prepare a staff list with work location, role, length of service and current wage components, then ask the responsible adviser to review the payroll implications.

For budgeting, separate a scenario from an approved payroll change. Record the source and effective date beside each assumption, and keep any outstanding district decision visible. This lets a PT PMA director plan staffing costs without presenting an unverified calculation as a legal conclusion.

---

## What This Does Not Establish

This update does not determine the wage due to an individual employee or confirm the application of the hotel sector figure to every accommodation business. Do not use the reported UMP as a substitute for reviewing the signed decision and relevant local rules.
