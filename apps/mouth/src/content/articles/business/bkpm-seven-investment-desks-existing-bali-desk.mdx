## TL;DR

BKPM's seven-location network includes Bali, whose desk was inaugurated in January 2026. Coordination and supervision do not remove project compliance requirements.

---

## The Facts

BKPM's proposed seven-location Investment Desk network includes Denpasar, but the Bali desk should not be presented as awaiting its first launch.

Bisnis.com's 9 November report says the network comprises Batam, Denpasar, Medan, Semarang, Balikpapan, Makassar and Jakarta. It reports that Deputy Investment Minister Todotua Pasaribu inaugurated the Medan desk that day and that desks in Batam and Bali had already been formed. Bali's coverage is described as Bali, West Nusa Tenggara and East Nusa Tenggara.

The report gives a collective investment-realisation target of IDR100 trillion. That is a target, rather than realised investment or a promised benefit for any applicant.

BKPM's own release establishes that the Bali Investment Implementation Control Desk was inaugurated on 22 January 2026 with the Bali provincial government. Its stated role includes investment supervision and coordination between central and regional authorities.

---

## What the Bali Desk Is Intended to Do

The official January release identifies concerns including fictitious companies, missing environmental and building documentation, activities closed to foreign investment, and failure to meet investment requirements. It describes an operational approach to improving compliance and investment governance.

The November network discussion adds a licensing-coordination context. It should not be read as an exemption from the substantive requirements applicable to an investment.

Neither source establishes a guaranteed processing time for a particular permit. This article also does not verify a walk-in address, appointment procedure or new service-level commitment for Denpasar.

---

## In Practice

A business with a delayed OSS application should first establish the actual reason for delay. Missing documents, an incorrect business activity, a spatial issue and an inter-agency coordination problem may require different responses.

A coherent case file is more useful than a general request to accelerate the licence. Preserve the application reference, submission history, outstanding requests and the agency's written responses.

## Bali Zero Take

### Our Analysis

The desk's value should be assessed through what it can coordinate in a specific case. Its supervision mandate also matters: an investor seeking help should expect questions about the underlying business and project documents.

For a proposed Bali development, preparation should include checking that the planned activity matches the company, land use and applicable approvals. An investment facilitation initiative does not establish that an otherwise unsuitable project can proceed.
