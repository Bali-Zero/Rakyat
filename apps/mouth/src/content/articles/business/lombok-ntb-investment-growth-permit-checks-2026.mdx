## The Facts


Bali-based investors looking toward Lombok and the wider West Nusa Tenggara province should read investment headlines alongside the practical requirements of their own projects.

## What the latest report says

DetikBali reported on 18 November that NTB's cumulative investment realisation for January–September 2026 reached Rp46.10 trillion, citing the provincial investment authority.

That is a cumulative nine-month figure, not investment generated in the third quarter alone. It is also a province-wide total, not a measure of villa returns or the performance of every tourism project.

The report covered a discussion in Mataram on 17 November concerning a draft governor's regulation on investment incentives and facilitation. Hospitality association representatives also raised concerns about complicated permitting.

## A draft remains a draft

The discussion does not establish that the proposed regulation is already in force or that a particular applicant qualifies for an incentive. Investors should look for the final official instrument and its conditions before relying on a proposed benefit.

Likewise, a report of permitting difficulties does not prove that every application is delayed or that all investors face the same problem.

## A practical expansion file

For a Bali business considering a project in NTB, a useful preparation file would bring together the proposed activity, ownership structure, exact site, current project documents and the authority responsible for each unresolved question.

Record what is already approved and what is still an application, proposal or commercial assumption. If a representative is handling the process, ask for the application reference and written status updates.

These are editorial due-diligence suggestions, not a statement that the same documents apply to every project. A successful operation in Bali does not, by itself, demonstrate that a separate NTB project is ready to operate.

## Read the investment number carefully

Provincial investment growth can provide economic context. It cannot establish the return, legal readiness or approval timeline of an individual resort, property or business.
