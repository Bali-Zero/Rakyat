## The Facts

Bali's economy grew 5.85% year on year in the third quarter of 2026, according to BPS Bali figures reported by Antara on 5 November. Accommodation and food-service activity grew 6.23%, compared with 10.94% in the corresponding quarter a year earlier.

Growth continued, but the pace in that tourism-related sector slowed. Slower growth is not the same as a fall in the absolute level of activity.

The same report recorded construction growth of 8.91%. These are provincial economic indicators, not estimates of the rental return or occupancy of an individual villa.

This article revisits data released on 5 November. It is not a new statistical release on 17 November.

---

## In Practice

A Bali business plan should connect the broad indicators to the customers and costs of the specific operation. A restaurant, a long-stay rental and a luxury hotel may serve different markets even when located in the same regency.

Review monthly revenue, booking lead times, cancellations, average customer spend and operating costs. Compare the same period across years where reliable records exist, rather than comparing a peak month with a quiet one.

For a new project, ask which assumptions are supported by local evidence and which remain forecasts. Include a scenario with slower revenue growth or a later opening.

## Bali Zero Take

### Our Analysis

A positive provincial growth figure is context, not a business guarantee. It can coexist with pressure on particular operators or locations. Equally, one business's weak month does not establish that the whole Bali economy is contracting.

Keep the denominator and comparison period visible whenever using a statistic. Provincial output growth, tourist arrivals and property income measure different things.
