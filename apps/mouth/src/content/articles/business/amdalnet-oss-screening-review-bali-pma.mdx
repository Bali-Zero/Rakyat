## The Facts

Indonesia is reviewing the environmental-screening framework that links Amdalnet with OSS business licensing. Hukumonline reports that the work concerns Permen LHK No. 4/2021 and Permen Investasi dan Hilirisasi No. 5/2025, with the aim of making document requirements better reflect the scale of a business activity.

This is a report about revisions under preparation. It does not establish that a replacement screening table has taken effect, or that an existing company can automatically downgrade its environmental obligations.

The official legal record for Permen LHK No. 4/2021 identifies the framework covering AMDAL, UKL-UPL and SPPL. These are different environmental-document routes; the relevant route must be checked against the actual activity and applicable criteria.

---

## In Practice

For a PT PMA planning a Bali operation, prepare one consistent description of the project before asking which document is needed. Record the business activity, proposed location, land and building area, production or service capacity, and any supporting facilities. Check that the figures match the licensing application and the plans used by the environmental adviser.

A villa business with supporting facilities should not rely only on a marketing description such as “small accommodation”. Ask the adviser to explain which activities and project dimensions were used in the screening, which rule supports the result, and whether the current application reflects all phases of development.

## Bali Zero Take

### Our Analysis

A system update is a reason to check records carefully, not a reason to assume approval. Keep a dated copy of the screening result and any written clarification. If OSS and Amdalnet show inconsistent requirements, record the discrepancy and use the official support channel rather than changing project figures merely to obtain a preferred result.

These are practical preparation steps, not a determination of which environmental document a particular business must obtain.
