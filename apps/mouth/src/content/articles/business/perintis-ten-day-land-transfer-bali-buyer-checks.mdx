## The Facts

The ATR/BPN legal registry identifies Ministerial Decision 1803SK-HR.01VIII2026, dated 12 August 2026, as the decision on Peralihan Hak Terintegrasi, or PERINTIS, 10 Hari. This is an existing August initiative being discussed again, not a regulation first issued in November.

ANTARA reported on 28 October that the ministry said the service was operating in 131 land offices. That is evidence of a rollout, not proof that every transaction at every office is covered. The relevant land office and the PPAT handling a particular transaction remain the practical starting points for confirming its route.

---

## In Practice

Before agreeing a completion schedule, ask your PPAT to explain which transfer is being processed, whether the responsible office accepts it through PERINTIS, and what makes an application complete. Request a written list of outstanding documents and the point at which the service target starts.

Separate preparation time from processing time. A headline service target does not tell a buyer how long it will take to resolve discrepancies in names, documents, payments or the proposed transaction. Build the contract timetable around the actual file and professional advice, rather than a headline alone.

Foreign buyers and PT PMA directors should also identify the legal holder, the type of land right and the intended use. A faster administrative process is not an answer to those underlying eligibility questions.

---

## What This Does Not Establish

This article does not promise a ten-day end-to-end purchase, certify eligibility for a specific Bali office, or suggest that the programme changes foreign landholding rights. Confirm the local service conditions before relying on the target.
