## The Facts

Bank Indonesia’s 18 November 2026 release states that its 17–18 November Board of Governors meeting increased BI-Rate by 25 basis points to 6.00%. It also lists the Deposit Facility rate at 5.25% and the Lending Facility rate at 6.75%. The central bank presented the decision as part of its stability policy.

BI-Rate is a policy rate. It is not a quotation for an individual mortgage, business loan or foreign-exchange transaction. The effect on a customer depends on the actual contract and the pricing offered by the provider.

---

## In Practice

For a Bali business, list borrowing by currency, fixed or floating rate, next repricing date and repayment schedule. Ask the lender to explain the contractual effect of any change before updating the budget. Keep fees and other charges visible rather than comparing headline interest rates alone.

An expat receiving foreign-currency income while spending in rupiah can review the timing of recurring expenses and transfers. Use a range of exchange-rate assumptions for planning instead of treating one central-bank decision as a prediction of the next currency move.

A PT PMA with imported equipment or overseas liabilities should distinguish operational currency needs from speculative positions. The practical task is to understand exposure and available cash, not to infer a guaranteed trading opportunity.

## What This Does Not Establish

This report does not predict the rupiah’s direction, recommend a trade or imply that all retail loans now cost 6%. Obtain a current quotation and review your own financing terms.
