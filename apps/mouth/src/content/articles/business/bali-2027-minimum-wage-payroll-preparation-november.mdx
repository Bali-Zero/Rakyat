## The Facts

Pojok Bali reported that the provincial wage council was discussing the proposal with representatives of government, employers and workers. It described a planned gubernatorial determination on 21 November. That is a reported timetable, not proof that a final amount has already been issued.

This update does not announce a new wage figure. We have not established a final 2027 Bali wage decision from the sources reviewed for this article. Figures discussed publicly or used in simulations should remain clearly labelled as proposals.

## A useful preparation exercise for a PT PMA

Our recommendation is to prepare the information needed for a payroll review now. Create a current staff list showing work locations, roles, employment dates and the components of remuneration. Record which document the payroll team currently uses as its reference.

Prepare several budget scenarios without describing any of them as the official minimum. Keep assumptions visible so the calculation can be updated when the applicable decision is available. This is an internal planning exercise, not a substitute for the decision itself.

---

## Before changing payroll

Ask your payroll adviser to identify the applicable official instrument, geographic scope, effective date and employee circumstances. Keep the source document with the calculation. A headline about the provincial wage alone does not settle every question about a particular employee's pay.

Give managers one consistent explanation: planning is underway, while any adjustment will be checked against the applicable decision and employment arrangements. Avoid distributing an unconfirmed figure as a company announcement.

---

## What to watch next

Look for the signed decision and its accompanying explanation through official provincial channels. If an announcement arrives, check its date and scope before updating budgets. This article records the discussion stage as reviewed on 20 November 2026.
