## The Facts

## Start with evidence of submission

The Q3 2026 LKPM reporting window has closed. For Bali PT PMA owners reviewing their compliance files, the immediate question is whether the company's July–September report was actually submitted and whether any follow-up remains outstanding.

DPMPTSP Central Kalimantan's 7 October announcement set the reporting window at 1–15 October 2026 through OSS for medium and large businesses. DPMPTSP Sintang's 1 October guidance independently identifies 15 October as the deadline for the July–September reporting period. These are official reminders about an existing reporting obligation, not a newly introduced October requirement.

## If the report was submitted

Retrieve the submission confirmation from the company account and keep it alongside the report itself. Check the reporting period, company identity, relevant project or business activity, submission date and current status. Ask the person who prepared the report to explain any correction request or unresolved message shown in OSS.

Reconcile the figures with the company's supporting records. Sintang's guidance identifies realised investment, workforce information, operating progress and supporting administrative data as areas to prepare. The reported information should reflect the actual stage of the business, including preparation or commercial operation.

A useful internal handover contains the report, the receipt, the supporting figures and a named person responsible for follow-up. An email saying that filing was “handled” is less useful than evidence that can be checked against the account.

## If no submission can be confirmed

Establish whether the report remains a draft, was submitted under a different project entry, or was never filed. Record the current account status and prepare the accurate Q3 information before requesting assistance.

Contact the official OSS support channel or the competent DPMPTSP for the procedure applicable to the company. The notices reviewed do not establish a blanket extension or guarantee that every late submission can now be entered in OSS. Do not treat an adviser’s assurance as confirmation that the filing has been accepted.

The official guidance warns of administrative consequences for late reporting. It does not establish that every missed deadline automatically produces the same sanction. Any warning or instruction received by the company should be reviewed on its own terms.

## Prepare the next reporting cycle

Sintang's published quarterly schedule places the Q4 deadline on 15 January of the following year. Set an internal preparation date ahead of that deadline and assign responsibility for collecting records during October–December.

For a Bali PT PMA, the practical priority is a traceable reporting file: accurate figures, evidence of submission and documented follow-up. Check the company's actual OSS obligations and project entries instead of relying only on a general reminder.
