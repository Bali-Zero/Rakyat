## The Facts

Bali Observer reported on 19 November 2026 that Supartha made the remarks on 17 November during a discussion with university students at the provincial council. Its report cites Bali Post and NusaBali; the linked Bali Post report dated 17 November also describes the discussion. He highlighted development pressure in Munggu and Pererenan, questioned permit compliance for construction around rice fields including Jatiluwih, and called for protection of community access to coastal areas.

He urged government agencies and customary villages to strengthen oversight. His comments about village rules, land conversion and coastal development are attributed concerns and proposals. The report does not announce a new province-wide investment ban, a new application deadline or a finding that every property in those areas is unlawful.

## Start with the exact location

A neighborhood name cannot establish the status of a specific parcel. Our practical recommendation is to assemble the land reference, boundary information, proposed activity and project address together before reviewing a purchase or lease proposal. Ask the relevant professional to identify any disagreement between the documents and the site.

The official OSS portal treats spatial suitability, environmental approval and buildings as separate basic-requirement areas. This makes it useful to ask separate questions about each part of a project, instead of treating one business document as an answer to every location and construction issue.

---

## Questions for an investor's document review

Ask the owner or representative to identify the authority under which they are offering the property. Have your notary or other appropriately qualified adviser examine the relevant land and contract documents.

For a proposed business, describe the actual intended activity: living in a building, constructing it and operating accommodation are different plans to discuss. Ask which spatial and building records apply to that plan and whether the information in those records matches what exists or is proposed.

Check how residents, guests and service vehicles would reach the property. If access crosses another parcel or coastal area, request the documentary basis rather than relying only on a sales photograph or a verbal promise. The news report alone cannot settle an access dispute.

Where a local or customary rule is said to apply, ask for the text, territorial scope and an explanation of its relevance to the particular transaction. This article does not assert that every lease requires identical customary approval.

## What this means for a Bali PT PMA

For a company preparing a project, appoint one person to collect unanswered questions and obtain written responses from the appropriate parties. Keep marketing statements separate from the documents supporting the transaction. A statement about general investment demand is not evidence that a specific site is suitable for your intended use.

These are practical review suggestions, not a determination that a named property has violated a rule. The appropriate conclusion depends on the actual parcel, activity and documents.
