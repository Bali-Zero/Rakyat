## The Facts

## Ask for the application route before paying for the result

For an investor preparing a project in North Bali, one useful question is simple: where will each application be submitted, and how will you check its progress? A promise to handle everything is not a substitute for that explanation.

Dewatapos reported on 15 October 2026 that Buleleng Regent I Nyoman Sutjidra called for greater digitalisation and transparency in land, spatial-planning and licensing administration. His remarks were a policy statement, not an announcement of a new permit law or guaranteed processing deadline. [Dewatapos report](https://www.dewatapos.co/berita/3737/bupati-sutjidra-tekankan-digitalisasi-dan-transparansi-perizinan-untuk-dukung-investasi-di-buleleng).

Buleleng already provides the official SI AJAIB online licensing portal. Its services include application submission, verification, progress tracking and electronically signed outputs. The local DPMPTSP also directs applicants to official online services. [SI AJAIB](https://siajaib.bulelengkab.go.id/) and [DPMPTSP guidance](https://dpmptsp.bulelengkab.go.id/faq) are useful starting points.

## Map each permission to its own channel

Do not assume every document for a development is processed in one system. Business licensing uses [OSS](https://oss.go.id/); building-related PBG and SLF applications use the [SIMBG system](https://simbg.pu.go.id/dashboard). Check with the competent authority which regional services and supporting requirements apply to the proposed project.

Bali Zero recommends preparing a table that names each intended document, the authority, the official channel and the evidence required to show completion. Ask a representative to explain any uncertainty before work begins.

A business-registration document, a building document and a submission receipt should not be treated as interchangeable proof. Record exactly what each document demonstrates and what remains outstanding.

## Make the engagement inspectable

Before appointing someone to manage applications, agree on the scope of work, fees, expected deliverables and the way progress will be reported. Separate professional fees from any official charges and request supporting receipts.

Ask for the application reference generated by the relevant system. Keep your company's authorised access arrangements clear, and avoid relying on screenshots that hide the application identity or status. Preserve copies of submitted forms and responses.

A legitimate representative can help organise documents and communicate with the responsible authority. A consultation or meeting is not automatically evidence of wrongdoing. The concern is an unexplained payment, an unverifiable application or a promise of approval outside the proper process.

## Check the project description before the paperwork moves

Give the representative a consistent account of the location, intended use, legal entity and planned activities. If the proposal changes, document the change rather than letting different applications describe different projects.

For a property transaction, ask the relevant professionals to identify which documents exist, which are still applications and which require further review. Do not treat the regent's transparency statement as proof that a particular plot, building or business is compliant.

## A practical handover checklist

Request an organised folder containing the application references, submitted documents, authority responses, receipts and final outputs. Add a short list of unfinished steps and the person responsible for each one.

Digital tools make a process easier to inspect only when the underlying records are available. For investors, the immediate action is to establish that visibility before handing over the licensing work.
