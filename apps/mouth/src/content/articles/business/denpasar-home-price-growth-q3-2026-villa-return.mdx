## The Facts

Bank Indonesia’s third-quarter 2026 residential property survey reports annual primary-market residential price growth of 0.78% for Denpasar. November reporting by Radar Bali discusses the same headline figure in the context of BI’s Bali residential survey.

The measure concerns a surveyed residential market and a defined period. It should not be relabelled as the return on every Bali villa, a forecast of future prices or a measure of rental income. The geographic label in the national survey is Denpasar; it should remain visible when using the figure.

---

## In Practice

When a sales presentation quotes a property statistic, ask four questions: which market, which property type, which period and which measure? Annual price growth, rental yield and total investor return answer different questions.

For a particular villa, build a separate cash-flow model covering the actual acquisition or lease arrangement, operating revenue, management costs, repairs, taxes and periods without guests. Test assumptions against relevant evidence for that location and business model rather than applying a city-level index to the property.

Keep any projected resale value explicit as an assumption. A historical index can provide context, but it does not establish the price at which a specific asset can be sold or the terms on which the investor can exit.

---

## What This Does Not Establish

The 0.78% figure is historical survey information, not a guaranteed return, a current valuation or investment advice. This is an educational reading of the data rather than a newly announced property rule.
