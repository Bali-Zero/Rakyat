## The Facts

Choosing how to enter Indonesia involves both business licensing and tax analysis. This is an educational checklist, not an announcement of a new regulation.

DJP's guidance explains that entities established or domiciled in Indonesia are domestic corporate tax subjects. It also identifies permanent establishments used by foreign entities to conduct business in Indonesia. A permanent establishment is treated similarly to a corporate tax subject for taxation purposes.

The official permanent-establishment regulation examines business premises and the activities carried out through them. It also addresses categories such as certain construction projects, services and dependent agents. A tax-treaty analysis may affect the result for an eligible foreign business; one threshold should not be applied indiscriminately across jurisdictions.

A representative-office label is not a blanket tax exemption. DJP expressly includes representative offices among the forms that can be relevant to permanent-establishment analysis. The actual activities and applicable conditions need to be examined.

---

## In Practice

Before deciding between a PT PMA, a representative-office arrangement or a team engaged through a service provider, prepare a short operational map.

Identify who signs customer contracts, who issues invoices, which entity receives revenue, where work is performed and who directs the local team. Record the premises used, how long activities last and what authority local personnel have.

Using an employer-of-record provider or independent contractors should not be treated as proof that the foreign business has no Indonesian tax presence. The contracts and operating facts still need review. This is a practical inference from the activity-based analysis, not a statement that every outsourced hire creates a permanent establishment.

For a PT PMA, separately review income-tax registration, bookkeeping, withholding and payroll responsibilities, and any VAT registration or invoicing obligations. Foreign ownership alone is not a sufficient explanation of a company's VAT position; DJP's guidance distinguishes taxable activities and applicable small-business thresholds.

## Bali Zero Take

### Our Analysis

A structure chosen for convenience can leave unanswered questions about the actual business. Conversely, a locally incorporated company does not make every payment or activity automatically compliant.

Use the operational map to obtain a written analysis tied to the relevant entity, activities and treaty, if applicable. Avoid relying on a generic comparison chart that describes a representative office as tax-free or an outsourced team as tax-exempt.
