## The Facts

Indonesia's Manpower Ministry has said the government has not appointed a manager for the proposed severance reserve fund, according to reporting published on 15 November by ANTARA and Bisnis.com.

The reports attribute the clarification to senior ministry official Indah Anggoro Putri. They say the circulating claim that employers must now transfer the reserve to Danantara is incorrect, and that implementation arrangements still require further regulation.

For a Bali hotel, restaurant or PT PMA, the distinction matters: a headline naming a future fund manager is not enough information on which to make a payment or change payroll procedures.

What to prepare now

Keep employment records, length-of-service information and existing benefit arrangements organised. Ask the person responsible for payroll or employment compliance to identify the actual published instrument behind any proposed change, including its effective date, affected employers and payment instructions.

If an intermediary asks for a transfer, verify the recipient and legal basis independently before acting. This is a practical document check, not a conclusion that existing obligations to employees have disappeared.

The new development is the ministry's clarification about the manager and implementation arrangements. The cited clarification does not provide a new contribution amount or payment deadline for employers to apply.
