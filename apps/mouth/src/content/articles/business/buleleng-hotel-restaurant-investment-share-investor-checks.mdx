## The Facts

Hotels and restaurants accounted for 78.2% of Buleleng's investment realisation in the third quarter of 2026, according to a 26 October report by Visi Bali quoting the regency's investment and licensing office, DPMPTSP. The figure concerns the two sectors together, not hotels alone.

The report attributes the assessment to DPMPTSP head Gede Ngurah Dharma Seputra. It says domestic investment rose in the second quarter and remained elevated in the third, while foreign investment was relatively stable. These are reported trends; the article does not provide the absolute rupiah totals needed to calculate a growth rate independently. [Read the Visi Bali report](https://visibali.com/2026/10/26/iklim-investasi-buleleng-menguat-hotel-dan-restoran-dominasi-realisasi-triwulan-iii/).

There is an important evidence boundary. Bali Zero has not independently reconstructed the 78.2% figure from Buleleng's underlying investment table. It is presented as an attributed statement from the local report, not as our own calculation.

## What investment data can tell you

BKPM's official third-quarter dataset explains that investment realisation is compiled from LKPM reports. Its published dimensions include sector, project location and investment origin. This helps distinguish reported investment activity from a sales forecast. The dataset's public description alone does not independently establish the specific Buleleng percentage above. [BKPM dataset and methodology](https://data.bkpm.go.id/dataset-detail/data-realisasi-investasi-triwulan-iii-tahun-2026).

A sector's share of reported investment does not measure a particular hotel's occupancy, a restaurant's profitability or the price appreciation of a plot of land.

## Bali Zero Take

### Our Analysis

For an investor considering northern Bali, the useful question is whether the proposed project works at its actual location and operating cost. A regional investment headline can help frame that investigation; it cannot answer it.

Do not infer that foreign investors face an easier licensing process, that approvals are faster, or that a spatial-plan revision is imminent. None of those conclusions follows from the reported sector share.

---

## In Practice

Before committing to a hotel, accommodation or restaurant project, request a project-specific evidence pack. It should identify the proposed operator, exact site, business activity and current status of the relevant documents.

Ask for a written explanation of unresolved licensing or location issues. Keep that separate from the seller's commercial forecast. An adviser should assess the actual operating model rather than assume that every hospitality activity follows the same route.

Build the financial model from dated quotations: land or lease terms, construction or fit-out, staffing, utilities, maintenance, insurance and working capital. Test a lower-revenue scenario and a delayed opening. These are planning recommendations, not predictions about the Buleleng market.
