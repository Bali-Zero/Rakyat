## The Facts

Property-market commentary attributed to Leads Property Services Indonesia CEO Hendra Hartono contrasts two approaches to investment: acquiring existing assets for earlier cash flow, and building a longer-term recurring-income portfolio.

A secondary report reproducing Kompas coverage attributes the discussion to an interview on 13 November 2026. It associates these approaches with Chinese and Japanese investor activity in Indonesia. These are an analyst's observations, not rules that describe every investor of either nationality.

This article does not use the unverified national investment totals in the earlier draft, and does not treat national commentary as evidence of Bali property prices.

## In Practice

For a Bali property decision, the more useful comparison is between business models. An operating asset may offer records to inspect; a development project may require assumptions about completion, future demand and operating costs.

For an operating property, request evidence supporting occupancy, room rates, expenses and net income. Reconcile the presentation with underlying records and distinguish owner forecasts from actual performance.

For a development, identify the payment milestones, funding assumptions, delivery timetable and remedy for delay. Ask how the investment performs if completion or occupancy falls short of the sales forecast.

## Bali Zero Take

### Our Analysis

A quicker route to revenue is not automatically a lower-risk route. Existing buildings can need repairs, and a long-term plan can still depend on optimistic assumptions. The investor's nationality is less useful than the asset, contracts, financing and management evidence.

An expat or PT PMA should compare like-for-like scenarios: the same time horizon, realistic costs, reserves and an exit assumption that can be explained. This is a due-diligence framework, not a recommendation to acquire a particular asset.
