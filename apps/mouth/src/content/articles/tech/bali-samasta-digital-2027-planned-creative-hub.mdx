## The Facts

## A proposed hub, not an open application

At the opening of Bali Digifest 2026, officials outlined plans for Bali Samasta Digital, with a target of use in 2027. The Bali DPRD secretariat's 12 November account places the proposal within the province's wider creative-economy agenda.

Bali Post's coverage describes revitalising existing government facilities for activities such as learning, training, business incubation, production, community use and exhibitions. The announcement also discusses developing the broader institutional framework for the creative economy.

The important distinction is timing: this is a plan and target, not evidence that the hub is already operating. The available reporting does not establish an exact opening day.

## Why it interests Bali's business community

A shared place for creative production, training and business development could be relevant to local studios, designers and technology businesses. Its practical value will depend on the programme, facilities and access arrangements that are actually delivered.

A project announcement cannot yet answer whether a particular business will be accepted, what services will cost or whether a foreign-owned company can join.

For expat founders and PT PMA operators, those questions are especially important. Nothing in the reviewed announcement establishes automatic eligibility, a funding entitlement or a tax incentive.

## Questions to track next

Before making a commitment based on the project, look for official answers on:

- The implementing organisation and confirmed location.
- Which facilities will open, and when.
- Application procedures, participant criteria and any fees.
- Whether opportunities are intended for individuals, companies or specific groups.
- The terms attached to any support that is eventually offered.

These are questions for future programme documents, not requirements already announced.

## Avoid turning a target into a guarantee

A 2027 target is useful context for planning, but it should not be represented as a guaranteed opening date. Proposed institutional or regulatory arrangements should also remain labelled as proposals until their status is independently confirmed.

Businesses can follow the project while continuing to assess their present operating needs. Do not postpone an essential workspace or business decision on the assumption that public facilities or financial support will become available on a particular day.
