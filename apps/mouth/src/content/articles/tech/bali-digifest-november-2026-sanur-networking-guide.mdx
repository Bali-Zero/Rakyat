## The Facts

## The event is underway in Sanur

Bali Digifest is scheduled for 12–14 November 2026 at Prime Plaza Hotel Sanur in Denpasar, according to its official website. This article is prepared on 13 November, during the advertised event period.

The official programme brings together conferences, an exhibition, competitions and creative activities, including film, animation, workshops and music. The site advertises free entry and provides registration and programme information.

For business owners, freelancers and creators, this offers a timely reason to look at the programme and decide whether a specific session is relevant. Free entry should not be read as a guarantee of unrestricted access to every activity; check the registration instructions for the session you intend to attend.

## Make the visit useful

Start with a concrete aim. You might want to understand a production workflow, meet a potential collaborator or explore tools relevant to an existing business.

Choose sessions against that aim rather than relying on a broad description of “digital opportunities”. A concise introduction to your work and two or three specific questions can make conversations more useful.

For a Bali-based PT PMA, attending a public business event does not itself establish eligibility for a grant, incentive or government programme. Ask each programme organiser about the actual terms before making plans around it.

## Practical checks before leaving

- Verify the latest rundown on the official event website.
- Confirm whether your chosen activity needs advance registration.
- Check the venue and allow time for the journey to Sanur.
- Bring a clear contact method and a short explanation of the collaboration you seek.
- Record any follow-up commitment accurately rather than treating an initial conversation as an agreement.

This is a visitor-planning guide, not an endorsement of individual exhibitors or a promise of investor introductions.

## Keep the event separate from future projects

Officials have also discussed longer-term digital-economy initiatives. Those plans are distinct from the event currently taking place. An exhibition stand or announcement at Digifest does not prove that a future hub is already open for applications.

The immediate opportunity is to review the programme, attend relevant sessions and verify specific offers directly.
