Files here:

  font that apps/mouth already ships (packages/core/fonts/files).
- MontserratTab-{500,600,700,800}.ttf: the same instances with the tabular-figures
  (tnum) feature on by default and renamed MontserratTab, so that invoice amounts
  align in columns. They are modified versions under the OFL and carry its
  copyright line unchanged.

The fonts are embedded (subset) in each generated invoice PDF by ReportLab and
are never served on their own.
